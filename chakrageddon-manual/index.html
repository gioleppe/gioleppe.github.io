Chakra-geddon: the manual

You wake up on a beach in Goa. Your passport is gone, your phone is dead, and a man with a beard like a mangrove is asking how you feel. The festival has been going on for longer than anyone can remember, and the creatures dancing in the incense are not, strictly speaking, real. They are Astrals, and you are going to need some.

Chakra-geddon is a satirical creature-collecting RPG that runs in your browser, on phones and on PC. Catch Astrals, win eight wristbands from the festival's DJs, gurus and influencers, foil Team Undercover's plan to sell the sunset, dance your way down the Last Dancefloor and climb the Mainstage Temple to face the VIP Shaman Council.

Play it now​

The game is free and runs at gioleppe.github.io/chakrageddon. It works offline once it has loaded, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

A word before you go

Chakra-geddon is a satire of festival culture. In the game, getting high changes what you see and how you fight. In real life, look after yourself and each other: drink water, know what you are taking, and find the welfare tent.
