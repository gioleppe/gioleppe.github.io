7. Influencer Chloe, the Sunset Cliff

Goal: beat Influencer Chloe for the VIP Press Pass. Suggested level: 27–28.

Getting there​

Back to the Flea Market. At the south exit, walk into the padlocked VIP fence with a Stimulant Astral in your party: Jaw Grind chews straight through. South of it is the Dry Ice Corridor, and south of that the Sunset Cliff.

The Dry Ice Corridor​

A maze of shipping containers and dry-ice foggers, indoors. The trainers here are a step up (Lv 25–27).

TrainerAstrals
Guard DsouzaShadowcop Lv 25, Sirenowl Lv 25, Bribe-asaurus Lv 27
Dealer MoeDengoth Lv 25, Nitroxide Lv 25, Mozzquito Lv 26
Influencer JaxSelfiend Lv 26, Glowkid Lv 26, Selfiend Lv 27

The Shadowcop in the fog​

In the middle of the maze, a shape made of torch beams blocks the fog and rubs two fingers together. It wants Rs 500.

  • Pay, and something small and sheepish drifts out of the light: a wild Shadowcop, Lv 25.
  • Refuse (or be too broke), and it swells with indignation and grows a hat: a wild Bribe-asaurus, Lv 29.

Either way it happens once, and you can try to bag what comes out.

Items: 2 Amber Vials on the west side, a Masala Chai in the north-east corner, and 2 Electrolyte Sachets (hidden) in the north-west corner.

Wild: Selfiend, Glowkid, Nitroxide and Shadowcop (Lv 25–28), Dengoth (Lv 26–28), and rarely Ravelord (Lv 30).

The Sunset Cliff​

"This sunset is brought to you by a brand partner." A clifftop over the sea with a stage and a ring light, a crowd facing the sea with their phones out, and a fence along the west edge with a sign: LOOKING WEST, RS 2000.

  • A Chai Tent in the south-west, shut until Chloe is beaten.
  • The Sunset Watcher, sitting on a rock in the south-west, has watched the sunset through the gaps in the fence every evening. He will sit with you until 6 PM if you ask, which matters later for Sunraga.
  • The Sunset Content Villa, the door in the north-west building. Inside: Assistant Pippa (Selfiend Lv 28, Lobsterlad Lv 28, Bongo Bishop Lv 29), Undercover Memo 2 and a Banana Lassi.
  • Exits: north back to the Corridor, west down a path to Fishermen's Cove (free, any time), and east past banyan roots to the Load-In Road (needs the Ash Mark).

Trainers​

  • Influencer Bex near the villa: Selfiend Lv 26, Bongo Bishop Lv 26.

Items​

  • Wraparound Shades: at the west end, by the cliff path. While you carry them, trainers spot you from one tile less.
  • Weighted Blanket: in the north-east corner.
  • Neon Glowstick: up on a ledge in the north, behind a subwoofer. Shove it out of the way with the Bass Drop Shove, but think before you push: shoved straight in, it jams. If that happens, leave the cliff and come back, because pushed things roll back when you leave.
  • Neon Glowstick: out over the sea in the south-east, on the stairs of light that only exist while your ego is gone. If you come down while you are standing out there, you wash up on Driftwood Beach.

Wild Astrals​

Selfiend (Lv 23–25), Lobsterlad, Bongo Bishop, Tridog, Nitroxide and Neighbula (Lv 23–25). During the Sponsored sunset (6 to 8 PM): Sirenowl (Lv 20–24).

The boss: Influencer Chloe​

"Hi guys! So today we are at the most EXCLUSIVE sunset in Goa..."

AstralTypeLevelWeak to
SelfiendStimulant / Bummer25Dissociative (four times)
GurnagamaStimulant25Dissociative

RavelordStimulant / Psychedelic26Bummer

Her moment: "Chat is going WILD." She plays to her followers and her Astral gets +1 Fractal power and +1 Vibe attack.

Strategy:

  • Her whole team is Stimulant. Dissociative moves take three of them apart, and Selfiend takes four times the damage. Neighbula, Wonkrab, Wooklet or Grand Wook, or your Hoffmanite later.
  • Ravelord is the exception: only Bummer moves hit it hard. Sirenowl, Dengoth or Bribe-asaurus.
  • Organic and Downer Astrals take extra damage from Stimulant moves. Keep Chillem and friends away from this stage.

Reward: Rs 3200 and the VIP Press Pass.

What the wristband unlocks​

Share Taxi Tuk-Tuk: open the Map tab and choose a Chai Tent you have rested at, and a tuk-tuk takes you there, with a short ride over the night road. It does not run from the Mainstage Temple. The driver at the Flea Market is suddenly very friendly.

The sadhu guarding the Sadhu Cave's mouth on the Sea Shallows now lets you in: "A press pass. Go in. Mind your head, and your sense of self."

Rematch

Chloe offers a rematch once you have all eight wristbands: Selfiend and Gurnagama Lv 37, Bongo Bishop Lv 38, Ravelord Lv 40.
