5. Chef Raju, the 5 AM Omelette Stall

Goal: beat Chef Raju for the Extra Cheese Stamp. Suggested level: 23–24. The Back Alley on the way is a gauntlet of seven trainers at Lv 19–22, so arrive ready for a long fight.

The Festival Timetable​

With three wristbands, the next time you walk into a new area Banyan jogs up waving a crumpled piece of paper. It is the Festival Timetable: a Sets page in your Fanny Pack and pink markers on the map. From now on sets play at fixed times, crowds fill the stage, and some Astrals only come out during a set.

Any Chai Tent will now offer to let you sleep until a set starts. Two night trainers also appear: Night Owl Suki on the Sandy Trail and Insomniac Theo in the Flea Market (Duvetghast Lv 25, Snoozel Lv 26, Neighbula Lv 26), both from 10 PM to 5 AM. They are tough for now.

tip

The Night Market (Flea Market, 5 to 8 PM) has a night vendor selling Amber Vials, Mystery Powder, Warm Kingfisher, Banana Lassi, Neon Glowsticks, Electrolyte Sachets and the Glow Jacket. Mystery Powder turns a Glowkid into a Ravelord, which is a big upgrade at this point.

Getting there​

In the Flea Market, walk into the subwoofer in the east gap with a Downer or Organic Astral in your party: the Bass Drop Shove pushes it through. Behind it is the Flea Market Back Alley, and the Omelette Stall is on the far side.

The Flea Market Back Alley​

Three dark yards of shipping containers joined by one-tile gaps. The first time you walk in, from either end, a shutter rattles down behind you. Constable Shah (hibiscus shirt, moustache stuck on crooked) explains that the exit is closed for a routine inspection. Of the air. Then he inspects your Astrals.

Both ways out stay shut until you have beaten all seven of Team Undercover, in any order. There is no tent in here, so pace yourself and bring healing items. The quest log tracks it as The Back Alley.

Most of them are Bummer: Dissociative moves are the answer (Mozzquito and Dengoth take four times the damage), and Downer moves work on the pure Bummers. Inspector Desai also hands over a confiscated Mystery Powder.

When the last one falls, a whistle blows, the moustaches come off, and the whole team files out through a door in a container: "WE WERE NEVER HERE." From then on the yards are full of people getting high.

Item: 2 Water Bottles (hidden) in the far south-east corner.

The 5 AM Omelette Stall​

"Open at 5 AM. It is always 5 AM." The stall is the second hub, in the east of the map.

  • A Chai Tent in the east, shut until Raju is beaten.
  • A local with a hint: "Raju's kitchen is all Organic and Downer. Stimulants cut through both."
  • Exits: west back to the Alley, north through fairy lights to the Bamboo Thicket (Machete Hack), and south past banyan roots to the Backstage Container, which you cannot climb yet.

Rival: Astra-Zero, round two​

Chad is waiting just inside the west entrance, and he has been shopping.

Items​

  • Weighted Blanket: in the south-east corner.
  • Masala Chai: in the south-west corner.

The boss: Chef Raju​

His moment: "Special of the day! A second helping for my little one." His Astral heals 30% of its health.

Strategy:

  • Stimulant moves hit all four, and Lassiblob and Chillem take four times the damage. Jawling's line, a Glowkid or Ravelord, a Djembe-Monk or a Selfiend are all good.
  • Bummer moves destroy Shroomba and Omeletron. A Mozzquito, Shadowcop or Sirenowl covers that half.
  • Leave your Dissociative and Downer moves at home: Shroomba and Omeletron take only a quarter from Dissociative, and the two Downers resist both.
  • Save a big hit for after his heal.

Reward: Rs 2000, an Electrolyte Sachet and the Extra Cheese Stamp.

What the wristband unlocks​

Pink Flamingo Float: with a Dissociative Astral in your party, you can walk on foam pits and shallow water. That opens two ways to the Forest Stage: north from the Scooter Ditch onto the Arabian Sea Shallows, or north from the Omelette Stall through the Bamboo Thicket, whose channel you can now float across.

Four wristbands also satisfy the lifeguard on Driftwood Beach: Fishermen's Cove is open (next chapter).

Rematch

Raju offers a rematch once you have six wristbands: Shroomba and Lassiblob Lv 29, Chillem Lv 30, Omeletron Lv 32.
