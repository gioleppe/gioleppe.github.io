12. After the credits

You have your passport back. The festival goes on, and so does the game: the goal line now just says "fill the Trip Journal". Here is what is left to do.

Rematches​

Every wristband boss offers one stronger rematch, with the same half-health moment. Talk to them again once you have enough wristbands. Most of these open up before the credits.

The strategies from each boss's chapter still apply: only the levels have changed. If you ask Diabolo outside his set, he tells you to come to the Pit at twenty-two hundred. "Do not be late, I start without anyone."

The Last Drop: Bassilisk​

Winning all eight rematches wakes something under the speaker stacks Backstage. The Promoter's quest, The Last Drop, tracks it. Go to the Backstage Container and look at the humming speaker stack in the north-east: Bassilisk, Lv 50, the strongest wild Astral in the game. See Legendaries.

The other legendaries​

  • Mandalord, from The Unfinished Mandala, if you did not finish it before the temple.
  • Sunraga, at sunset on the Sunset Cliff, once Team Undercover is exposed and only while you are Peaking.
  • Vicchiomorph, after The Aliens of Vicchio.
  • Mirrorling and Lucidra, in the water, only with your ego gone.

All of them are on the Legendaries and one-of-a-kinds page.

Filling the Trip Journal​

There are 41 Astrals. Banyan rewards you each time you show him a new milestone on Driftwood Beach:

BaggedBanyan gives you
105 Rizla Papers
203 Amber Vials
303 Masala Chai
All 41Rs 5000 and 2 Mystery Powder

"Every single one. The whole state of mind of this festival, in one duct-taped notebook. I think I can finally go home. I will not, but I could."

The ones people miss:

  • Your two other starters and their middle forms only appear while you are Peaking: Ganjapod on the beach, Jawling on the Sandy Trail, Blottertot in the Poi Pit, Fractoad in the Tantric Yurt, Hashlump at Sweetwater Lake and Gurnagama at the Forest Stage. Their final forms: Hoffmanite (Sadhu Cave, Ego gone), and Charagolem and Amphetadragon on the Last Dancefloor.
  • Sets: no Astral is set-only, but sets are the earliest way to meet some of them: Bribe-asaurus at the Night market, Ravelord at Diabolo's headline set and Omeletron at the 5 AM rush, all at Lv 20–24, long before they turn up in the wild. See the timetable in chapter 5.
  • Night-only: Shadowcop and Mozzquito show up on the early routes only at night (they are wild by day further on).
  • Evolutions: Lobsterlad, Duvetghast and Ravelord can also come from a catalyst (Warm Kingfisher, Banana Lassi, Mystery Powder) instead of a level.

The full table, with every location and level range, is in the Astral Journal. On the Map tab, an eye marks any zone where a high-only Astral you have not seen yet is still hiding.

Side quests still open​

Anything you skipped is still waiting. The full list is on the Side quests page. The most rewarding ones to mop up after the credits:

  • The Crab Rave: the Crab Suit and a crab dance.
  • Peak Experience: a Nitroxide.
  • Lost Crates: a Neon Glowstick, or Rs 1500 if you keep one.
  • Power Cut: Earplugs and the Generator Disco.

Being high, properly​

With nothing left to lose but rupees, this is a good time to see what Ego gone does to the world. Your reflection may challenge you on the beach or the trail, a memory vendor who looks like you sets up in the Flea Market, the cow walks off into the jungle, Banyan's diary has a page you have never seen, and stairs of light lead out over the sea from the Sunset Cliff.
