11. The Mainstage Temple

Goal: climb the ziggurat past the Four-on-the-Floor and your rival, and face the VIP Shaman Council. Suggested level: 42–44.

The ziggurat​

You land on warm stone. Behind the stage, the Mainstage Temple goes up in terraces into the dark. On the first terrace a kick drum starts; on the second, a bassline; on the third, a hi-hat ticking like a clock; on the fourth, a melody you feel you have always known. At the very top, three figures stand against the stars and look down at you. "Climb, then. Everybody wants to be at the top. Very few want to climb."

The temple is a terraced ziggurat: a plaza at the foot, then five terraces, each narrower and higher than the last, with the Council on the summit. Each terrace's stair is one tile wide, and someone stands at the top of every stair. Nobody can walk round them. There are no wild Astrals here, and no tuk-tuk will take you away from it.

The plaza​

  • A Chai Tent in the west. It works until your first fight with the Four. Once you have beaten one of them, Chai Mama refuses until you have beaten all four: "You started. Finish, then sit, and I will pour."
  • The Water Auntie next to it. The first time you talk to her on a climb, she heals your whole party ("you look like a raisin. Drink. All of you."). After that her chai is finished, but her water jug never is: she refills your Astrals' Hydration as often as you like.
  • A roadie in the east sums up what is coming: "Kick, bass, hat, lead. Heavy, wobbly, fast, clever. Then whoever is sulking in the last doorway."
  • Items: 3 Masala Chai in the east and 2 Weighted Blankets in the south-west corner.
  • The back stairs at the foot of the plaza lead down to the Last Dancefloor's side gate.
The Four are one climb

If you white out partway up, the climb starts over: all four are back at their posts and the Water Auntie's chai is full again. Stock up on Masala Chai, Weighted Blankets and Electrolyte Sachets before your first fight (the Backstage Bar and the Merch Stall sell them), and rest at the plaza tent before you start.

When you beat one of the Four, they walk up through their door and stand aside on the next terrace, cheering you on.

  • Selfiend: Dissociative (four times). Duvetghast: Stimulant or Organic. Omnihound: Downer (four times). Ravelord: Bummer. Grand Wook: Psychedelic (four times).
  • His ace is your starter's rival, fully evolved: Amphetadragon takes four times the damage from Dissociative moves; Hoffmanite is weak to Psychedelic, Organic and Downer; Charagolem takes four times the damage from Stimulant moves.

He pays Rs 8000, then stands aside on the Council's terrace. "Whatever. Go on, then. They are waiting. ...Good luck, I guess. Do not tell anyone I said that."

The VIP Shaman Council​

The summit, in gold light. The Elder, the Seer and the Bead-Beard hum in harmony. This is not a fight.

"So. The barefoot one. Eight wristbands, four terraces, and one very well dressed ego, all behind you."

They tell you the truth about your passport, which you may already suspect: it fell out of your pocket on the beach, and they kept it safe. They could have just handed it back, but then you would have gone home on Tuesday with a clean shirt and no stories. They give you the Passport. Your return flight left six days ago. Or it leaves tomorrow. Time is not their department.

Then you choose:

  • Go home: "A sensible choice. Banyan made the same one in 1994. He is still packing."
  • Two more weeks: "Two more weeks. Yes. That is what they all say. That is what we said."

Either way the credits roll, then the end card. You wake up on Driftwood Beach, healed, next to Banyan: "Ah, you are back. With a passport! Put it somewhere safe. Not too safe." The festival goes on, your Journal still has blank pages, and, he adds, there is something in the Sadhu Cave that has been watching for a very long time.

On to After the credits.
