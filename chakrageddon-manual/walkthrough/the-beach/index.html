1. Driftwood Beach and the Sandy Trail

Goal: get a starter from Professor Banyan, catch a second Astral, and walk north to the Poi Pit. Suggested level by the end: 6–7.

The dream​

The game opens in a dream. You are lying on warm sand at 4 PM and a white-bearded face with pupils like dinner plates is hanging over you. He explains the basics while you are out cold: Astrals, battling, bagging, Hydration and Serotonin, the blotter in your fanny pack, and the eight Wristbands of Enlightenment that get you in to see the VIP Shaman Council.

He asks who you are (Moonbeam, Dave or Ziggy) and lets you ask him one question. None of the answers change anything. "Where are my shoes?" gets the most honest reply.

Then you wake up for real, face-down in the sand. Your backpack, your shoes and your passport are gone. In their place: a fanny pack, half a bottle of warm coconut water and a Glowing Blotter.

Professor Banyan and your starter​

Walk over to the old man by the shack. Professor Banyan came to Goa in 1994 for two weeks. He tells you the Council has your passport, and that you need all eight wristbands to get in. Then he offers you one of the three Astrals from his stash, all at level 5.

Whichever you pick, your rival turns up with the one that was meant to beat it. Pick Ganjapod and he brings a Jawling, whose Stimulant moves hit Organic Astrals twice as hard: that is the toughest start of the three. The two starters you did not pick are not gone for good: they hide in the early zones and only show themselves while you are Peaking (see Peak Experience).

Banyan also gives you 5 Ziploc Baggies and 3 Coconut Water, explains the four levels of high (Microdose, Come-up, Peaking and Ego gone), and takes down the festival tape across the beach path. Two tips from him worth remembering: hold B to run, and do not stand still too long with the blotter in your bag, or you will start licking it out of boredom.

Driftwood Shack Beach​

The beach is your home base for the whole game.

  • Chai Tent (east side): Chai Mama heals everything and keeps your stash.
  • Banyan's Shack (the door in the north-west): his hammock heals and becomes your whiteout point. The diary on the table gives you a Masala Chai the first time you read it, and there is a pickup of 2 Water Bottles inside.
  • Beach Stall (the vendor in the middle): Ziploc Baggies, Coconut Water, Water Bottles, Tiger Balm, and two outfits (Tie-Dye Shirt, Cotton Kurta).
  • Water Bottle on the sand in the south-east, by the waterline.
  • The Crab Raver on the west of the beach starts The Crab Rave. It takes the whole game to finish, so just say hello for now.
  • While you are Peaking, a Glowing Stranger stands on the sand to the east. Sober, you cannot see them at all. The first time they give you 2 Amber Vials.
  • The lifeguard on the rocks at the east end turns you back: Fishermen's Cove is round there, but he wants to see four wristbands first.

Wild Astrals in the incense clouds: Crabcake (Lv 2–3), Ketacrab (Lv 2–4) and Snoozel (Lv 2–3). While Peaking, Ganjapod (Lv 3–4) turns up too.

tip

Catch something here. Ketacrab is a Dissociative crab, the one type DJ Diabolo's Djembe-Monk is weak to. Wear one down, then throw a baggie.

The Sandy Trail​

Head north up the beach path. The Sandy Trail winds north for a good while:

  • South of the creek: tide pools in the west and a drum circle round a bonfire in the east, with a yogi sitting on the logs. Four incense clouds to catch in.
  • The creek: one plank bridge, and the Fire-Spinner watches the far end of it, so you cannot sneak past her.
  • North of the creek: dunes, a surfboard stuck in the sand, more incense, and a funnel of dunes and palms leading up to the Poi Pit. Your rival watches the funnel.
  • The north exit: Ranger Asha.

Ranger Asha​

If you are carrying only one Astral, the ranger will not let you through: "Diabolo's crowd will eat you alive. Bag one more in the incense first." Talk to her and she gives you 3 Ziploc Baggies, once. With two Astrals she waves you on, and once Diabolo is beaten the way is always open.

Wild Astrals​

  • Day: Crabcake, Djembe-Monk, Tridog and Snoozel (Lv 3–5), Shroomba (Lv 4–5). Jawling (Lv 4–6) while Peaking.
  • Night (7 PM to 6 AM): Mozzquito (Lv 3–5), Tridog (Lv 4–6), Snoozel (Lv 3–5) and Shadowcop (Lv 4–5).

Items​

  • Watermelon Slice: by the drum circle in the south-east.
  • Water Bottle (hidden): in the tide pools in the south-west. Hidden items only show once you have the Quartz Wristband and a Psychedelic Astral; come back for it later.
  • 2 Ziploc Baggies: on the far west edge, north of the creek.
  • Lost Sandals: in the east, north of the creek. They belong to a tourist at the Sunrise Guesthouse (The Lost Sandals). Pick them up now and hand them in when you reach the Flea Market.
  • Ziploc Baggie: in the dunes in the north-east.

Rival: "Namaste" Chad, alias Astra-Zero​

The guy from the seat next to you on the charter flight now calls himself Astra-Zero. Brand-new harem pants, a trust fund and a store-bought Astral picked to beat yours. He blocks the funnel on the way to the Poi Pit and you cannot walk round him.

Your starterHis Astral
GanjapodJawling, Lv 5 (Stimulant)
JawlingBlottertot, Lv 5 (Psychedelic)
BlottertotGanjapod, Lv 5 (Organic)

He pays Rs 250. With Ganjapod, switch in your second catch if Jawling is hurting you; with Blottertot, your Psychedelic moves hit his Ganjapod twice as hard.

Then go north into the Poi Pit.
