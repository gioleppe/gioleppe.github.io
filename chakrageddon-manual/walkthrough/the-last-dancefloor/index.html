10. The Last Dancefloor

Goal: cross the Mainstage crowd, beat the Front Row, and get yourself crowd-surfed onto the temple. Suggested level: 40–41.

"Between the Backstage and the Mainstage: ten thousand people, and every one of them is in your way."

This is the victory road. It runs a long way south from the Backstage's velvet rope to the stage at the bottom, and it is built in bands: a chill-out ring at the top, then three bands of dancing lanes, with packed crowds and trainers in between. There is a Chai Tent at the top, so you can always walk back up to heal.

How the dancing lanes work​

The lanes are rows of dancers. With every step you take, each row's dancers move one tile along it, in groups with gaps. Some rows go faster, some slower, some the other way.

  • You cannot walk into a dancer. Try, and you wait a beat while the row moves on, so you can never be boxed in.
  • Nobody ever dances onto the tile you are standing on.
  • So: step into a gap, watch the next row, step again. "Patience is a dance move too."

The packed crowds between the lanes are encounter areas, like the Poi Pit's.

The Outer Ring​

The top of the field, just south of the Backstage.

  • The welfare tent in the north-west, with a Chai Tent.
  • Ziggy, by the welfare tent, has lost her friend Noor in the crowd: Lost in the Crowd.
  • The Merch Stall in the north-east: Amber Vials, Rizla Papers, Weighted Blankets, Electrolyte Sachets, the Glow Jacket and the Sequin Vest. "Official merch! Unofficial merch! The difference is the price and nothing else."
  • A sign explaining the lanes.

The middle: the FOH island​

Between the first and second bands of lanes, Marta's sound desk sits in its pen in the middle of the field, with packed crowds either side. Marta mixes the Mainstage, and she explains how it works: "The Council only let in people the crowd carries. There is no stairway, no guest list. There is the crowd, and the crowd decides." Beat the Front Row at the barrier and the crowd will lift you.

The light towers​

Between the second and third bands. Noor is here, in the east, dancing on exactly the spot she promised not to leave.

The Front Row​

At the bottom, past the third band of lanes, is the barrier in front of the stage. A one-tile run leads to the only gap in it, and the Front Row stand guard in alcoves looking into the run. They have been here since the gates opened.

  • Dex is mixed: Dissociative moves hit Amphetadragon four times as hard; Psychedelic moves hit Grand Wook four times and Hoffmanite twice; Organic and Downer moves also hit Hoffmanite.

A security guard at the west end of the front explains the side gate: it is the temple's back stairs. "You can come out this way. You cannot go in this way. Going in is up to the crowd."

Items​

  • Water Bottle x3: east side, middle section, just below the first lanes.
  • Warm Kingfisher: in a clearing in the west crowd, middle section.
  • 2 Amber Vials: far west edge, middle section.
  • Masala Chai (hidden): in the clearing in the east crowd, just beside Darkpsy Superfan Vlad.
  • 2 Weighted Blankets: far west edge, by the light towers.
  • 2 Electrolyte Sachets: east side, by the light towers, right next to Noor.
  • 2 Tiger Balm: far east edge at the front, below the third lanes.

Wild Astrals​

The packed crowds hold the strongest wild Astrals in the game: Ravelord, Omnihound, Grand Wook and Selfiend (Lv 35–38), Amphetadragon and Charagolem (Lv 36–38), and Dengoth (Lv 35–37). This is the only place to catch a wild Amphetadragon or Charagolem.

The crowd-surf​

Until both of the Front Row are beaten, the crowd will not lift you: "The Front Row are still watching you, arms folded. The crowd takes its cue from them."

Beat Kiki and Dex, heal at the welfare tent if you need to, and step into the gap in the barrier. Somebody behind you shouts your name. Then everybody does. A hundred hands lift you off your feet and pass you forward over the heads (somebody steals one of your sandals; it is worth it), and the last row throws you, quite hard, up over the lip of the stage.

After that, coming back through the gap gives you a short version: the crowd knows you now, and passes you up "like a parcel". The temple's back stairs bring you out at the side gate here, so you can come back down to heal or shop at any time.
