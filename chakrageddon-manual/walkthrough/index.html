Walkthrough

Spoilers ahead

This walkthrough covers the whole game, from waking up on the beach to the VIP Shaman Council and everything after the credits. Every boss team, every secret Astral and the ending are here. If you would rather find things out for yourself, use the Places and Astral Journal pages instead, and only peek here when you are stuck.

How to use this walkthrough​

The game tells you what to do next. The goal line under the zone name and on the Map tab always points at the next wristband boss, and the route hint says which exit to take. This walkthrough follows that same route, one chapter per wristband, and adds what the hints leave out: the trainers on the way, the Astrals worth catching, the items lying around (including the hidden ones), each boss's team with a plan for beating it, and the side quests that open along the way.

Each chapter has the same parts:

  • Getting there: the exits and gates, and which field technique opens them.
  • The areas on the way: trainers, wild Astrals and pickups.
  • The boss: their team in a table, their half-health trick, and a strategy.
  • What the wristband unlocks.

Side quests are only mentioned where they start. The full steps are on the Side quests page. The secret Astrals have their own page: Legendaries and one-of-a-kinds.

The route at a glance​

  1. Driftwood Beach and the Sandy Trail: Professor Banyan, your starter, the first rival fight.
  2. DJ Diabolo, the Poi Pit: the Hemp String Wristband.
  3. Guru Svetlana, the Tantric Yurt: the Quartz Wristband, and the Anjuna Flea Market.
  4. Captain Keta, the Sunken Scooter Ditch: the Wonky Keychain, past a very sacred cow.
  5. Chef Raju, the 5 AM Omelette Stall: the Extra Cheese Stamp, after the Back Alley and the second rival fight.
  6. Baron Von Darkpsy, the 190 BPM Forest Stage: the UV Skeleton Band, across the sea and the lake. Fishermen's Cove opens.
  7. Influencer Chloe, the Sunset Cliff: the VIP Press Pass and the tuk-tuk.
  8. Baba Smoke, the Sadhu Cave: the Ash Mark.
  9. The Promoter, the Backstage Container: the All-Access Hologram, and the Load-In Road.
  10. The Last Dancefloor: the victory road, the Front Row and the crowd-surf.
  11. The Mainstage Temple: the Four-on-the-Floor, the rival, the Council and the ending.
  12. After the credits: rematches, legendaries and a full Journal.

The world​

The festival sprawls along the coast of North Goa. On the region map (the Map tab in your Fanny Pack) it looks like this:

  • The south-west is where you start: Driftwood Shack Beach, with Banyan's shack. The Sandy Trail runs north from it to the Poi Pit, and the Tantric Yurt sits north of the Pit.
  • The middle is the Anjuna Flea Market, the first hub, with the main shop and the Sunrise Guesthouse. Four roads leave it: west to the Yurt, north up the Clogged Dirt Road (with the Jungle Shortcut and the Roadside Shrine as detours round the cow), east through the Back Alley, and south into the Dry Ice Corridor.
  • The north is water: the Sunken Scooter Ditch at the top of the Dirt Road, then the Arabian Sea Shallows with the Sea Grotto on its islet and the Sadhu Cave in the cliffs behind. Arambol Sweetwater Lake lies to the east.
  • The east is jungle: the 190 BPM Forest Stage south of the lake, the Dense Bamboo Thicket (and the hidden Vicchio Crop Circle), and the 5 AM Omelette Stall, the second hub.
  • The south is the business end: the Sunset Cliff below the Dry Ice Corridor, Fishermen's Cove between the cliff and the beach, the Load-In Road east of the cliff, the Backstage Container, the Last Dancefloor, and the Mainstage Temple in the far south-east corner.

Field techniques​

Each wristband teaches a field technique. Techniques never take a move slot: they work by themselves when you walk into the obstacle, as long as you have the wristband and an Astral of the right type in your party. This is why the bosses are also the keys to the map.

Chai Tents​

Chai Mama's tents heal your whole party and your stash, sober you up, and become the place you wake up after a whiteout. There are tents on Driftwood Beach, in the Poi Pit, the Tantric Yurt, the Scooter Ditch, the Omelette Stall, the Forest Stage, on the Sunset Cliff, at the Last Dancefloor and at the foot of the Mainstage Temple. Banyan's hammock and the Sunrise Guesthouse bed (Rs 100) heal too.

In a boss's zone the tent stays shut until you beat the boss ("Win first. Then sit, and I will pour."), and so does any shop there. Heal up before you walk in.
