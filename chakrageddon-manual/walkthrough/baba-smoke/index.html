8. Baba Smoke, the Sadhu Cave

Goal: beat Baba Smoke for the Ash Mark. Suggested level: 33–34. This is a big jump from Chloe, and Baba brings five Astrals.

Juniper, the tarot reader​

With six wristbands, the next time you arrive somewhere, a see-through, violet-glowing Banyan floats down out of the sky. "Do not panic. My body is at home in the hammock. This is only my astral body." He tells you a card reader has set up a table at the Anjuna Flea Market.

Juniper (round glasses, mustard beanie) reads the past lives of Astrals. For Rs 500 an Astral remembers one move its line learns by its level that it does not know now: a move it forgot, skipped, or could have learned as an earlier form. Useful for getting a strong old move back before the big fights ahead.

Getting there​

Go back to the Arabian Sea Shallows (by the Scooter Ditch, the lake, or by tuk-tuk to the Scooter Ditch's tent and a short float). The sadhu at the cave mouth in the north now steps aside. Float across to the ledge under the cliffs and go in.

If you finished Low Tide, Rosa can also row you from Fishermen's Cove up to the Shallows' beach.

The Sadhu Cave​

"Silence. (Smoke permitted.)" A dark cave of tunnels, candles and smoke. Baba sits on his cushion at the far north end. There is no Chai Tent here: heal before you come, and bring plenty of Masala Chai.

On the east wall is a faded pattern, a circle inside a square cut into four, with a few grains of colour still clinging to it. It does nothing yet. Remember it.

Items: 2 Masala Chai in the north-east corner, and two hidden ones: a Mystery Powder on the west side and 2 Amber Vials in the south-east corner.

Wild: Chillem, Omeletron, Duvetghast, Grand Wook, Omnihound and Sirenowl (Lv 29–32). With your ego gone, Hoffmanite (Lv 31–33) too.

The boss: Baba Smoke​

Baba never says a word. The smoke over his head does the talking: a question mark, then a wristband.

His moment: "Breathe in. Deeper. Now you are the smoke." The battle gets a lot more intoxicated, so critical hits come more often for both sides.

Strategy:

  • Psychedelic and Stimulant moves between them cover everything. A Ravelord (Stimulant / Psychedelic, from a Glowkid and a Mystery Powder) is perfect: its Psychedelic moves hit Grand Wook four times as hard and Omeletron and Hoffmanite twice, and its Stimulant moves hit Chillem and Charagolem four times as hard.
  • Organic moves hit four of his five twice as hard (all but Omeletron).
  • A Bummer Astral handles Omeletron (four times), but Grand Wook and Hoffmanite's Dissociative moves and Chillem and Charagolem's Downer moves hit it hard.
  • Dissociative and Downer moves are mostly wasted here.
  • Five Astrals means a long fight. Bring Masala Chai and a Weighted Blanket or two.

Reward: Rs 3800 and the Ash Mark: not a band at all, but holy ash pressed round your wrist. It does not wash off. You tried.

What the wristband unlocks​

Banyan Root Climb: with an Organic or Psychedelic Astral in your party, you can climb banyan roots on cliffs. Three sets of roots matter:

  • The Omelette Stall's south exit: straight down to the Backstage Container. The shortest way to the last boss.
  • The Sunset Cliff's east exit: onto the Load-In Road, which also ends at the Backstage.
  • Fishermen's Cove's north-east corner: up to the Sunset Cliff.

The Unfinished Mandala​

Right after the fight, Baba points a long finger at the east wall, and the smoke draws the story: three figures in orange robes kneeling round the pattern with funnels of coloured sand, until a gust blows them away. Then he points at the wall, at you, and holds up four fingers.

This starts The Unfinished Mandala, the quest for the legendary Mandalord. You need four coloured sands from all over the map, and one of them only appears after the Lakshmi quest. Baba draws you a map of what is still missing every time you talk to him. It is best done now or after the credits; Mandalord is Lv 37.

The Inner Room​

With your ego gone, the pattern on the east wall holds itself open like a door. Step through into the Inner Room, where an old woman who taught Baba Smoke, back when he was "a skinny boy with a lot of questions and no shoes", sits on the floor. Pick up the Mystery Powder she leaves for you. When you come down, the room folds shut and you are standing in the cave again.

Rematch

Baba offers a rematch once you have all eight wristbands: Omeletron, Grand Wook and Chillem Lv 41, Hoffmanite Lv 42, Charagolem Lv 43.
