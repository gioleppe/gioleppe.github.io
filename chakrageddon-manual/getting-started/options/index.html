Options

Open Options from the title screen, or during play from the Fanny Pack: MENU › System › Options. Change a setting with A, or with left and right. A short explanation of the setting under the cursor is shown at the bottom of the screen.

Options are remembered on your device, separately from your save. They are not part of your trip, so you can change any of them at any time without consequences.

The settings​

SettingChoicesWhat it does
DifficultyEasy / Normal / HardHow hard everyone hits. See below.
Grounded modeOn / offCaps the trippy screen effects at their mildest level, however high you are. Recommended if you are sensitive to motion or flashing.
Reduced motionOn / offStops screen shake, flashes and spinning backgrounds. Scene changes become plain fades, and long animations become still pictures.
Text speedSlow / Normal / FastHow fast dialogue types out.
Battle speedNormal / Fast / Very fastHow fast battles play out. Holding B also fast-forwards.
Battle styleShift / SetWhat happens when you knock out a trainer's Astral. See below.
Picture sizeFull / Large / Medium / SmallShrinks the picture in sharp whole steps, centred on black. The help line tells you the current size in pixels.
Volume0% to 100%Music and sound together, in steps of 10%. 0% is mute.
Sound testListen to the music of every area, the battles, the bosses and the title.
Remap keyboardChoose your own keys. See Controls.
Export save to fileDownloads your save as a file, to keep as a backup or move to another device.
Import save from fileLoads a save file. This replaces the save on this device. Then choose Continue on the title screen.

Defaults are in bold.

Difficulty​

Difficulty changes only one thing: the damage moves do.

Your Astrals' movesThe other side's moves
Easyhit 20% harderhit 20% softer
Normalas they areas they are
Hardhit 20% softerhit 20% harder

Everything else stays the same: catching, prices, levels, water costs, how high you get. Damage from status conditions is not affected either. You can switch difficulty in the middle of your trip, even right before a boss you are stuck on. Nobody will know.

Battle style​

  • Shift (the default): when you knock out one of a trainer's Astrals, you are told which Astral they will send next and asked Swap Astral or Keep going. Swapping is free: your new Astral comes in before theirs does, and nobody takes a hit.
  • Set: the trainer sends their next Astral straight in, and you fight on with whoever is out.

Shift makes trainer fights noticeably friendlier. Set is for people who like it tense.

Grounded mode and reduced motion​

Being high in Chakra-geddon changes the picture: ripples, colour shifts, wobbling scanlines and, at the very top, ghost images and inverted colours on critical hits. It is a big part of the joke, but it is not for everyone.

  • Grounded mode keeps the picture at the gentle level you would see at a light Come-up, however high your character gets. You lose none of the gameplay: the rules of being high still apply, only the screen stays calm.
  • Reduced motion stops the movement: no shake, no flashes, no spinning title rays, no rainbow trails. It works on its own or together with grounded mode.

The boss cutscenes and the intro never flash, whatever you choose.

tip

You can turn both on and off whenever you are free to open the Fanny Pack (MENU › System › Options). If a particular area or trip is too much, switch them on, get through it, and switch them back.
