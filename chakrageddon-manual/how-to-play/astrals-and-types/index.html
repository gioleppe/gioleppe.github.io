Astrals and Types

An Astral is what a state of mind looks like when it gets loose: a sunburned tourist who believes he is a crab, a balloon full of laughing gas, the guy with the bongos next to your tent at 7:30 AM. There are 41 of them at the festival. Some live in the incense, some only come out at night, during a particular set, or when you are high enough to see them. A few are one of a kind.

The six types​

Every Astral has one or two types, and so does every move.

TypeWhat it feels like
PsychedelicVisuals, third-eye entities, sacred geometry.
StimulantHyper-speed, techno-gremlins, endless chatter.
DissociativeGravity-bending, noodle limbs, horse spirits.
OrganicEarthy, munchie beasts, chill golems.
DownerCouch-lock sloths, warm blanket ghosts.
BummerShadow people, siren noises, existential dread.

There is also a seventh kind of move, Normal, which has no type at all: it hits everyone the same, and never gets the same-type bonus. No Astral is Normal.

Types are always written out as words in the game, never shown by colour alone.

The type chart​

Read across from the move's type to the defending Astral's type.

2 means "It really resonates!" (double damage). ½ means "It barely registers..." (half damage). Everything else is normal damage.

A few things worth knowing:

  • Two types multiply. A move that is doubled against both of a defender's types hits four times as hard. One doubled and one halved cancel out.
  • Same-type bonus. A move that matches one of the attacker's own types hits 1.5 times as hard.
  • No type resists itself. A Psychedelic move hits a Psychedelic Astral normally.
  • The chart can flip. A few moves, and one boss, turn the chart upside down for a few turns: double becomes half and half becomes double. When everything feels backwards, it is.

In battle, once you have seen an Astral before, the move panel tells you whether a move resonates or is weak against it. On an Astral's summary, point at a type and press A for a reminder of what it beats and what beats it.

Stats​

Each Astral has six stats, shown on its summary page:

StatWhat it does
SerotoninIts health.
Vibe AttackHow hard its physical moves hit.
Ego DefenseHow well it takes physical moves.
Fractal powerHow hard its astral moves hit.
GroundednessHow well it takes astral moves.
BPMIts speed. The faster Astral moves first.

Hydration, the water for its moves, is a separate pool (see Battles).

Moves are physical (Vibe Attack against Ego Defense), astral (Fractal power against Groundedness) or status (no damage, something else happens).

Abilities​

Every Astral has one ability, the same for its whole evolution line. It works by itself.

Thick SkinTakes 20% less from physical moves.

StaminaEvery move costs one less Hydration.

GroundedCritical hits never land on it.
Sharp EyesLands critical hits more often.
BurstIts first move after coming out hits twice as hard. Then it pops.
MirrorCopies the first move used on it, for the rest of the battle.

The last two belong to one Astral each. You will know them when you meet them.

Levels and growth​

Astrals level up ("peak") by earning Vibes in battle, up to level 60. With each level they grow a little stronger, and now and then learn a new move.

Growth by chance​

Stats do not grow by a fixed amount. Every level-up rolls the dice:

  • Hydration grows by nothing (40% of the time), 1 (50%) or 2 (10%).
  • The other stats grow around their species' usual rate, sometimes a point more, sometimes a point less.

So two Astrals of the same kind at the same level are never quite the same. Each one keeps its own luck for life, through evolution too.

Each evolution line also has a growth schedule for each stat: some stats grow early, some late, some steadily. Everyone grows steadily up to level 10. Between 10 and 24 the early bloomers pull ahead, and after that the late ones catch up. Point at a stat on the summary page and press A: the note tells you how that Astral grows it.

tip

A late bloomer that looks a bit weedy at level 18 may well be the best Astral in your party at 35. Give it time.

Many Astrals change into a stronger form. Most do it by reaching a level, right after a battle they won or an Astral was bagged in. A few can also be pushed along with a catalyst item, used on them from the Stash Tin.

The starters' final forms pick up a second type: Charagolem is Organic and Downer, Amphetadragon Stimulant and Bummer, Hoffmanite Psychedelic and Dissociative.

A catalyst does nothing to an Astral that does not use it. Mystery Powder also gets you a little higher. Nobody will say what is in it.

Where to find them​

Your Trip Journal (in the Fanny Pack) keeps track. An Astral you have only seen gets a blue ballpoint sketch; one you have bagged gets its full picture and notes. Press A on any Astral you have seen and its Where page lists every place it lives, at what levels, and when: by day or night, during a set, on land or water, or only once you are high enough. See Bagging Astrals.
