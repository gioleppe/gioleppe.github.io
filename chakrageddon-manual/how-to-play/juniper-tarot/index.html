Juniper's Tarot

"I read past lives. Only for Astrals, though. People's past lives are all Cleopatra. It got boring."

Juniper is a tarot reader in round glasses and a mustard beanie, and her table smells of oat milk and palo santo. For a fee, she helps an Astral remember a move it has forgotten, skipped, or never got round to learning.

Finding her​

After your sixth wristband, the next time you arrive somewhere, Prof. Banyan drops in. Or rather, his astral body does: a see-through, violet-glowing Banyan who floats down out of the sky. ("My body is at home in the hammock. This is only my astral body. Very convenient. Terrible for the back.") He tells you a card reader has set up at the Anjuna Flea Market.

Her table is there from then on. The first time you sit down, she riffles an enormous deck by candlelight and turns over a card.

A reading​

A reading costs Rs 500: one Astral, one move remembered. "Cards don't lie, but they don't do refunds either."

  1. Talk to Juniper and choose A reading.
  2. Pick an Astral. Your party is laid out as tarot cards, each with how many moves it could remember. One with none has "its past all here already".
  3. Pick a move from its past. Every move is shown with a note on what it does.
  4. If the Astral already knows four moves, pick the one it forgets to make room.
  5. Confirm. The cursor starts on "No", so you cannot pay by accident.

Choose How does it work? for Juniper's own explanation, or Not now to leave. ("Mercury is in retrograde anyway.")

Which moves can it remember?​

An Astral can remember any move that:

  • its evolution line learns at or below its current level, including moves that only its earlier forms learn, and
  • it does not already know.

So a move learned at level 30 is not on offer to a level 25 Astral, but a move you skipped at level 12 is. A Charagolem can remember things it learned back when it was a Ganjapod.

When to visit​

  • You skipped a move when your Astral learned something new, and now miss it.
  • Evolution moved on and an old favourite from the first form is gone.
  • You need coverage: a move of another type, or a heal, or a status move, for a particular boss.
  • Water troubles: a cheaper move can keep an Astral fighting longer than a thirsty strong one.
tip

Before you pay, look at the Astral's moves on its summary page in the Fanny Pack (point at a move and press A). Then decide which one is worth Rs 500 to bring back, and which one can go.
