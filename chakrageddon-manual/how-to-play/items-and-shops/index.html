Items and Shops

Everything you carry lives in your Stash Tin (the bag page of the Fanny Pack). You start with Rs 300, a warm coconut water and a glowing blotter. Rupees come from beating trainers, finishing quests, and selling things you do not need.

Using items​

  • From the Stash Tin: pick an item. Healing items and catalysts ask which Astral to use them on; held items ask who should hold them; clothes go on (or come off); key items tell you what they are.
  • From Quick Items: press ITEMS (Q on a keyboard) on the map for a quick panel of snacks and drinks next to your party's health bars. See Controls.
  • In battle: choose BAG. Only bagging and recovery items can be used there.

The first row of the Stash Tin sorts it: By type, A to Z or Most first.

Gear​

Gear is used from the Stash Tin, or simply works while you carry it.

Catalysts​

A catalyst makes certain Astrals evolve on the spot. Use it from the Stash Tin on the Astral you want to change. It does nothing to the others.

Held items​

Give one to an Astral from its page on the Astrals tab (Hold item), or pick it in the Stash Tin and choose who holds it. Each Astral holds one item, and it works by itself in every battle. Take it back returns it to your tin.

ItemPriceEffect
Friendship BandRs 700Heals a sixteenth of its Serotonin at the end of every turn.
Neon GlowstickRs 900Its moves hit 10% harder.
Lucky RupeeRs 600A coin with a hole in it. More critical hits.
Steel FlaskRs 650Its moves cost a third less Hydration.
Sand Mala—Its holder keeps a clear head: no Thought Loops, no K-holes. A quest reward.

Several held items can also be earned by helping people around the festival.

Clothing​

Outfits are purely cosmetic: they change how your hero looks on the map and in their portrait. Put them on and take them off from the Stash Tin. Your bandana, shades and wristbands always stay on (hats hide the first two).

OutfitPriceWhere

Cotton KurtaRs 500Beach Stall.
Sequin VestRs 800Flea Market, Merch Stall. "Catches every laser in the valley."
Glow JacketRs 900Flea Market, Night Market, Merch Stall.
Festival Poncho—A thank-you from somebody at the Sunrise Guesthouse.
Crab Suit—A lilo and two oven gloves, from the Crab Raver.

Clothes are one of a kind. While you own an outfit, the shop shows it as "Sold out". Sell it back and it is in stock again. The same goes for held items.

caution

A whiteout costs you one random outfit. It walks off with somebody while you are out. A shop outfit comes back into stock, so you can simply buy it again. For a gift outfit, go back to whoever gave it to you: they always seem to have a spare.

Key items​

Key items are for the story and for quests. They never run out, cannot be sold, and choosing one in the Stash Tin reads it to you. They include:

  • Glowing Blotter: lick a corner to go up a band. It never runs out. See Intoxication.
  • Festival Timetable: Banyan's photocopy, folded into eighths. It opens the Sets page (see The Festival Timetable).
  • Passport: yours, slightly chewed. Eventually.
  • Memos, crates, coconuts, coloured sands, a bent antenna and other quest things. People will tell you what they are for.

Shops​

Talk to a stallholder to open their shop. Buy shows the stock, Sell shows what you can sell, Leave closes it. Each press of A buys or sells one.

Selling pays half the price. Key items, and things that were free, cannot be sold.

Free stuff​

  • Pickups lie around the festival. Walk up to one and press A. Some only show under a blacklight, some only at low tide, and some only when you are high.
  • Wild Astrals you beat sometimes leave a drink or a snack behind.
  • Prof. Banyan rewards a growing Trip Journal (see Bagging Astrals).
  • Quests pay in rupees, items and the occasional outfit. The Quests page in the Fanny Pack keeps track.
