The Fanny Pack

Your backpack is gone. What you have instead is a fanny pack, and it holds everything: your Astrals, your stuff, your notes and your sense of direction. Press MENU (M or Tab on a keyboard, Start on a gamepad) to open it, and MENU or B to close it.

The tabs run down the left side. Pick one with up and down and A, or tap it. Under the tabs you always see your rupees and a meter of how high you are.

Astrals​

Your party, in order: the one at the top goes out first in battle. Each row shows the Astral's level, its Serotonin and Hydration bars, and a tag if it has fainted (OUT) or has a status.

Pick an Astral for:

  • Summary: its full page (see below).
  • Move: change the party order. Pick an Astral, then the slot to swap it with.
  • Send to stash: only while you sit at a Chai Tent.
  • Hold item: give it a held item from your tin, or Take it back.
  • Nickname: pick one of the suggestions or type your own, up to 12 characters. Choose its own name to go back to it.

Below the party is the stash, sorted by order caught, name or level (tap the chip, or press left and right). Pick a stashed Astral to Take it, Swap with... a party Astral, or read its Summary. Taking and swapping only work at a Chai Tent.

The summary​

The summary shows an Astral's types, Serotonin, Hydration, how many Vibes until it next peaks (levels up), its stats, its four moves, its ability and its held item.

Point at anything on it (with the d-pad, or a tap) and press A, and it explains itself in plain words:

  • a move: its power, accuracy, water cost and every effect, with the odds;
  • a stat: what it does, and whether this Astral grows it early, late or steadily;
  • a type: what it hits hard and what hits it hard;
  • the ability and the held item.

This is the best place to learn the game's rules as you go.

Stash Tin​

Your bag, in a brushed-metal tin. The top row sorts it By type, A to Z or Most first. The item under the cursor shows its category and description below the list.

Pick an item to use it: healing items and catalysts ask which Astral; held items ask who should hold them; clothes go on or come off (the one you wear is marked "worn"); key items tell you about themselves; the blotter gets licked. Bagging tools wait for a wild Astral. See Items and Shops.

Journal​

The Trip Journal, a duct-taped notebook with a coffee ring on it. It lists all 41 Astrals: question marks for those you have not met, a ballpoint sketch for those you have seen, and the real picture and notes for those you have bagged. Press A on a seen Astral for its Where page (every place it lives, and when) and its Notes. Details in Bagging Astrals.

Map​

The top of the page shows your current goal ("Beat DJ Diabolo in the Poi Pit for the Hemp String Wristband.") and a hint on which way to go next ("Next: head north to the Poi Pit."). The same hint appears under the area's name whenever you arrive somewhere new.

On the drawn map:

  • Red squares are hubs; yellow squares are other places; grey ones you have not visited yet, shown as "???".
  • Your head bobs on the place you are in.
  • A red flag marks your goal.
  • A small tent marks every place where you have rested at a Chai Tent.
  • A yellow "!" means somebody there has a quest for you.
  • A pink marker means a set is playing there right now.
  • An eye means a high-only Astral still hides there.

Move the cursor between places with the d-pad, or tap one. Once you have the VIP Press Pass, place the cursor on a place with a tent you rested at and press A to ride a tuk-tuk there. See Travel.

Sets​

Appears once Prof. Banyan has brought you the Festival Timetable. It shows the festival clock and every set, in order of start time: the one playing now is marked LIVE, and sets starting soon say how long until they begin. The set under the cursor shows where it plays, until when, and what it is like. See The Festival Timetable.

Quests​

Your side quests. Active ones are listed with their next step; finished ones are greyed out underneath. Quest givers in the world show a "!" above their head, and people waiting for you to hand something in show a "?". "New quest" and "Quest complete" are announced as they happen.

Wristbands​

Your eight Wristbands of Enlightenment. The list is on the left; the one selected turns slowly in fake 3D beside it, each made of its own material. Under it: who gave it to you, a description, and what it lets you do. A band you have not earned yet is a dark silhouette with a question mark. See Wristbands and Techniques.

Log​

The last 80 lines of dialogue, battle messages and notices, in case something scrolled past too fast. "Nothing has happened yet. Give it time."

System​

  • Save writes your progress to this device. The game also saves by itself whenever you change area and after battles.
  • Options opens the settings (see Options).
  • Unstuck: walled in, or lost? This takes you back to where you came into the current area. Nothing else changes. The game asks first, with "No, stay here" on the cursor. Anything you pushed around in the area rolls back too.
tip

Unstuck is there for the rare moment you trap yourself, say behind a subwoofer you shoved the wrong way. It never costs you anything, so use it freely.
