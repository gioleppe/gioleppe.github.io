Astral Journal

All 41 Astrals in Trip Journal order: their types, ability, where to find them in the wild, and how they evolve.

How to read the "Where" column:

  • Plain entries are in the zone's incense, bush or crowd tiles, by day (and at night too, unless the zone has a night table of its own: see below).
  • (night): only from 7 PM to 6 AM. Six zones swap to a night table after dark: the Sandy Trail, the Poi Pit, the Tantric Yurt, the Anjuna Flea Market, the 5 AM Omelette Stall and Fishermen's Cove. In those zones, entries without "(night)" are daytime only.
  • (water): on water, which you can only reach with the Pink Flamingo Float (Chef Raju's wristband and a Dissociative Astral).
  • (Peaking) and (Ego gone): only while you are at least that high. These Astrals are invisible to sober eyes. A person in each of those zones drops a hint, and the Map marks the zone with an eye until you have seen what hides there.
  • (... only): only while that set is playing (you need the Festival Timetable, from the third wristband). Times are in chapter 5.
  • Peaking adds one level to wild Astrals, and Ego gone adds two.

For the six one-of-a-kind Astrals at the end, see Legendaries.
