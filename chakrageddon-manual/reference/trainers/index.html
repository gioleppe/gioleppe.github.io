The VIP Shaman Council​

The Elder, the Seer and the Bead-Beard, on the summit of the Mainstage Temple. Not a fight: a scene, your passport and a choice. See chapter 11.

Everyone else, zone by zone​

The number after each Astral is its level. Trainers who stand on a dancefloor dance until they spot you; some look around in turns instead of staring straight ahead. Once a trainer has seen you, there is no walking away: "There is no walking away from eye contact." Wraparound Shades make them notice you from one tile less.
