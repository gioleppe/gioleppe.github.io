Items and where to find them

Every item lying around the festival, zone by zone, plus the shops and the things people give you.

Marks used on this page:

  • (hidden): invisible until you have the Quartz Wristband (Guru Svetlana's) and a Psychedelic Astral in your party. Then the Blacklight UV Pulse shows them.
  • (low tide): in Fishermen's Cove, only reachable across the flats from 4 to 10, morning or evening.
  • (Ego gone): only reachable while your ego is gone.

Each pickup can be taken once. Directions are compass directions on the zone's own map (north is up).

Driftwood Shack Beach and Banyan's Shack​

  • Water Bottle: on the sand by the water, in the south-east.
  • 2 Water Bottles: inside Banyan's Shack.
  • Masala Chai: read Banyan's diary in the shack (once).

Sandy Trail​

  • Watermelon Slice: by the drum circle, south-east.
  • Water Bottle (hidden): in the tide pools, south-west.
  • 2 Ziploc Baggies: on the far west edge, north of the creek.
  • Lost Sandals: on the east side, north of the creek (The Lost Sandals).
  • Ziploc Baggie: in the dunes, north-east.

The Poi Pit​

  • Tiger Balm: in the east of the chill-out area, near the Fire Spinner.
  • 2 Watermelon Slices (hidden): in the south-east corner.
  • 3 Rizla Papers: just past the fairy lights behind the stage, in the north (after you beat Diabolo).

The Tantric Yurt​

  • 2 Watermelon Slices: on the west side, by a lamp.
  • Banana Lassi: in the north-east corner, beside the yurt.

Anjuna Flea Market and the Sunrise Guesthouse​

  • Electrolyte Sachet: on the west side.
  • 2 Incense Sticks: on the east side.
  • 2 Masala Chai (hidden): in the north-east.
  • 2 Rizla Papers: inside the guesthouse, in the east corner.
  • Undercover Memo 1: beat Undercover Grunt Ravi in the guesthouse.

Flea Market Back Alley​

  • 2 Water Bottles (hidden): in the far south-east corner.

Clogged Dirt Road​

  • Amber Vial (hidden): in the south-east, near the turn-off to the Roadside Shrine.

Jungle Shortcut​

  • Mystery Powder (hidden): on the west side, just north of the stream, by Dealer Raz.
  • 3 Rizla Papers: in the middle bends.
  • 2 Tiger Balm: near the top, on the west side.

Roadside Shrine​

  • 2 Incense Sticks: in the clearing north of the courtyard.
  • Masala Chai: on the west side of the courtyard.
  • Amber Vial (hidden): in the marigold garden, south of the courtyard.

The Sunken Scooter Ditch​

  • Warm Kingfisher: north-west.
  • Amber Vial (hidden): north-east.
  • 3 Rizla Papers: south-east.

The 5 AM Omelette Stall​

  • Masala Chai: south-west corner.
  • Weighted Blanket: south-east corner.

Fishermen's Cove and the Sea Cave​

  • 2 Water Bottles: in the Scramble, west.
  • Amber Vial: in a nook under the cliff, north-west.
  • 2 Coconut Water: in the palm grove, east.
  • Tiger Balm (hidden): among the rocks just west of the fishing village.
  • 2 Electrolyte Sachets (low tide): out on the sandbar.
  • 3 Rizla Papers (low tide): on the little western islet.
  • Mystery Powder: inside the Sea Cave, east side (low tide to get in).
  • Masala Chai: inside the Sea Cave, west side.

Arabian Sea Shallows and the Sea Grotto​

You need the Pink Flamingo Float for almost all of these.

  • 2 Water Bottles: under a towel at the south end of the west beach.
  • 2 Amber Vials: on the ledge below the Sadhu Cave's mouth, north.
  • 2 Coconut Water: on a sandbar, north-east.
  • Watermelon Slice: on the spit, south-east.
  • 2 Amber Vials (hidden): on a little sandbar in the south of the bay, west of the islet.
  • Cool Blue Clay: at the very back of the Sea Grotto, north-east (Mad Dogs and Englishmen).
  • Amber Vial (hidden): in the Sea Grotto's south-west corner.

Arambol Sweetwater Lake​

  • Banana Lassi: north-west corner.
  • Record Crate: north-east corner (Lost Crates).
  • 2 Electrolyte Sachets: on the island in the middle (float out).
  • 3 Watermelon Slices: on the east shore, in the south.

Dense Bamboo Thicket and the Vicchio Crop Circle​

  • 2 Electrolyte Sachets: south-west.
  • Electrolyte Sachet (hidden): south-east.
  • Record Crate: on the west side, south of the channel.
  • Warm Kingfisher: north-east, across the channel.
  • 2 Coconut Water: in the Vicchio Crop Circle, east of the circle (once the path is open).

190 BPM Forest Stage and the DJ Booth​

  • 2 Tiger Balm: south-west.
  • Record Crate: on the east side of the clearing.
  • Tiger Balm (hidden): north-west.
  • Mystery Powder (hidden): north-east corner.
  • 2 Electrolyte Sachets: inside the DJ Booth, west side.

Dry Ice Corridor​

  • 2 Electrolyte Sachets (hidden): north-west corner.
  • Masala Chai: north-east corner.
  • 2 Amber Vials: on the west side.

The Sunset Cliff and the Sunset Content Villa​

  • Wraparound Shades: at the west end, by the path down to Fishermen's Cove.
  • Weighted Blanket: north-east corner.
  • Neon Glowstick: up on the ledge in the north, behind a subwoofer you have to shove (Bass Drop Shove).
  • Neon Glowstick (Ego gone): out over the sea in the south-east, on the stairs of light.
  • Undercover Memo 2: inside the villa, west side.
  • Banana Lassi: inside the villa, east side.

The Sadhu Cave and the Inner Room​

  • 2 Masala Chai: north-east corner.
  • Mystery Powder (hidden): on the west side.
  • 2 Amber Vials (hidden): south-east corner.
  • Mystery Powder (Ego gone): in the Inner Room, behind the east wall.

The Load-In Road​

  • Banana Lassi: in the truck park, north-west, between flight cases.
  • 2 Amber Vials: in the far north-east, behind the containers.
  • Jerrycan: deep in the lighting store maze, south-west (Power Cut).
  • 2 Tiger Balm: in the lighting store maze.
  • Weighted Blanket: inside the generator yard.
  • 2 Electrolyte Sachets (hidden): in the far south-east corner.

Backstage Container and the Green Room​

  • 3 Amber Vials: north-east corner.
  • 2 Masala Chai: south-west.
  • 2 Electrolyte Sachets (hidden): among the flight cases on the east side.
  • Undercover Memo 3: in the Green Room's north-west nook, behind two subwoofers.
  • 2 Amber Vials: in the same nook.

The Last Dancefloor​

  • 3 Water Bottles: east side, just below the first band of lanes.
  • Warm Kingfisher: in a clearing in the west crowd, middle section.
  • 2 Amber Vials: far west edge, middle section.
  • Masala Chai (hidden): in the clearing in the east crowd, where Darkpsy Superfan Vlad stands.
  • 2 Weighted Blankets: far west edge, by the light towers.
  • 2 Electrolyte Sachets: east side, by the light towers, next to Noor.
  • 2 Tiger Balm: far east edge at the front.

Mainstage Temple​

  • 3 Masala Chai: on the plaza, east.
  • 2 Weighted Blankets: on the plaza, south-west corner.

Wild drops​

A beaten wild Astral leaves something behind about one time in twelve: usually a Water Bottle or Coconut Water, sometimes a Watermelon Slice or an Electrolyte Sachet, rarely a Tiger Balm. Bagging or running gives nothing, and neither do trainers (they pay in rupees).
