I'm Tommaso, an Infrastructure Architect and Software Engineer based in Florence. I enjoy solving problems across software and infrastructure, from building APIs to designing better ways to deploy them.

At Avanade, I lead an engineering team, help clients improve their systems and delivery practices, and share what I learn through workshops. Lately, I've been exploring generative AI and helping a client move to on-premises Kubernetes.

Previously, I worked on cloud infrastructure and software at YOOX NET-A-PORTER, Giuneco, and as a freelancer.

## What I work with

- **Technologies:** AI tools (Claude Code, Pi, GitHub Copilot), Azure, AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform, Docker, Azure DevOps
- **Languages:** C#, Python, Go, ...
- **Practices:** DevOps, policy as code, system design, cloud cost optimization

## Research and speaking

I have a BSc in Computer Science from the University of Pisa and am completing my MSc, expected in February 2027. I've co-authored research on [small language models for climate-report summarization](https://aclanthology.org/2024.nlp4pi-1.10.pdf) and [mobile crowdsensing](https://iris.cnr.it/bitstream/20.500.14243/499702/1/Centralized_Coverage_and_UAV_deployment%20(1).pdf).

I enjoy sharing what I learn through workshops and talks. I've given talks on Android reverse engineering and SearXNG.

## Beyond work

I'm interested in distributed systems, LLMs, functional programming, open source, and self-hosting. When I'm not writing code or reading a paper, you might find me listening to music by some unknown band.
