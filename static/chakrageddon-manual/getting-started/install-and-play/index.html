Install and Play

Chakra-geddon runs in your web browser, on a phone or a computer. There is nothing to download and no account to make. Once it has loaded, it keeps working offline, so it is happy on a beach with no signal.

Opening the game​

Go to gioleppe.github.io/chakrageddon in any up-to-date browser. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.

The very first time, a notice called Before you go in explains what you are getting into: it is a satire about a festival, full of jokes about drugs and the people who take them, meant for adults, and fiction rather than advice. It also warns that some scenes wobble and flash. You choose:

  • Play with effects: the full experience.
  • Play in grounded mode (steady picture): keeps the screen calm. You can change this any time in Options.

Then a short animated intro plays (it plays every time you open the game). Your first button press or tap turns the sound on; the next one skips the intro.

Installing it on a phone​

The game is a web app you can pin to your home screen. It then opens full-screen like any other app, and plays offline.

  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): open the browser menu (⋮), then Add to Home screen or Install app.

When a new version comes out, it downloads quietly in the background and takes over the next time you launch the game.

tip

On a phone held upright, the picture sits at the top and the controls below it. Turn it sideways and the controls float in the margins either side of the picture. Both work; pick whichever your thumbs prefer.

The title screen​

  • Continue picks up your saved trip. It only appears once you have a save.
  • New trip starts from the beginning. If you already have a save, the game asks you to press again before it overwrites it.
  • Options opens the settings (see Options).

Saving and loading​

There is one save per device, kept in your browser.

  • The game saves by itself every time you move to a new area and after every battle.
  • You can also save by hand at any time: open the Fanny Pack (MENU), go to System and choose Save.
  • Continue on the title screen loads it.

To move your trip to another device, use Export save to file in Options on the old one, then Import save from file on the new one.

caution

Your save lives in the browser's storage. Clearing your browsing data, or playing in a private window, can wipe it. If your trip matters to you, export a copy now and then.

How your trip begins​

You are lying on warm sand outside Driftwood Shack, passed out, while a white-bearded face blocks the sun. In this half-dream he explains the festival: it is full of Astrals, creatures that are what a state of mind looks like when it gets loose. People keep them as friends and battle with them. Wear a wild one down and it can be stabilised in a baggie. Serotonin is their health, Hydration their stamina. Eight festival bosses hand out Wristbands of Enlightenment, and with all eight the VIP Shaman Council will see you.

He asks your name (Moonbeam, Dave or Ziggy) and lets you ask him one question. Then you wake for real, at 4:00 PM, face down on Driftwood Shack Beach. Your backpack, shoes and passport are gone. In their place: a fanny pack, half a bottle of warm coconut water, and a suspiciously glowing blotter.

The old man is Prof. Banyan, who came to Goa in 1994 "for two weeks". Walk over to the shack and talk to him. He tells you your passport is with the Council at the Mainstage Temple, and offers you one Astral from his stash.

Choosing your starter​

All three start at level 5. Banyan lets you look at each one and change your mind before you commit.

StarterTypeIn Banyan's wordsWhat to expect
GanjapodOrganic"A sleepy coconut. Slow, sturdy, and it heals itself on the couch."Tough and patient. Its Couch Lock traps the foe and heals Ganjapod every turn. The hardest start, on purpose: Organic is weak to two of the common early types.
JawlingStimulant"A gecko that never stops talking. Fast and fragile."Hits first and hits often. Its Unsolicited Life Story strikes two to five times and can tip the foe into a Thought Loop.
BlottertotPsychedelic"A paper frog. Strange powers, and it bends the rules."A tricky one. Its Kaleidoscope Spit (learned at level 7) flips the whole type chart for a few turns.

There is no wrong answer, only a different trip. The two you did not pick can still turn up later, if you know how to look (being very high helps).

After you choose, Banyan hands you 5 Ziploc Baggies and 3 Coconut Waters, explains the four levels of high (see Intoxication), and takes down the festival tape that blocks the beach path. The first wristband is with DJ Diabolo at the Poi Pit, north along the Sandy Trail.

note

A ranger at the top of the Sandy Trail will not let you through to the Poi Pit while you carry only one Astral. Bag a second one in the incense clouds on the way. Talk to her and she gives you three baggies to help.
