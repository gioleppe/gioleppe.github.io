Controls

Chakra-geddon plays like an old handheld: a d-pad, an A button to confirm and a B button to go back. It works with a keyboard, a touch screen or a gamepad, and you can switch between them whenever you like.

Walking around​

  • Move with the d-pad. Walk into people and signs, then press A to talk to them or read them. On a touch screen you can also just tap the picture.
  • Run by holding B while you move. You cover ground twice as fast. You cannot run while floating on water.
  • Doors and exits are walked through. Walking off the edge of a map takes you to the next area.
  • Stand still for a few seconds and your hero starts dancing. Stand still for much longer, outdoors, and they start licking the blotter out of boredom. Banyan did warn you. See Intoxication.

Talking and choosing​

  • A moves dialogue on. If text is still typing out, A shows the rest of it.
  • When a question comes up, pick an answer with the d-pad and confirm with A. A question ignores your button for a split second after it opens, so a hasty double press does not pick something by accident.
  • Words in colour are special: key items in magenta, other items in amber, Astrals in teal, places in purple and game rules in blue.

Menus​

  • MENU opens the Fanny Pack, your main menu (see The Fanny Pack). MENU or B closes it again.
  • Up and down move through a list, A picks, B goes back a step. Left and right switch pages or change a setting where it makes sense.
  • Everything can be tapped or clicked. Tap a row to pick it. Very small rows take two taps: the first moves the cursor there, the second confirms.

Quick Items​

ITEMS (Q on a keyboard, X on a gamepad) opens a small panel without leaving the map: your snacks and water on the left, your party with their health bars on the right. Pick an item, then the Astral to give it to. Every item that heals or rehydrates turns up there by itself, and so does the blotter.

In battle​

  • Pick commands and moves with the d-pad and A; B steps back.
  • Hold B to fast-forward the play-by-play. Important lines (a boss's words, a change in how high you are) still wait for you to press A, so you never skip them by accident.
  • MENU during a battle shows the log of everything that just happened, laid over the fight.

Remapping keys​

Keyboard keys can be changed in Options › Remap keyboard. Choose an action, then press the key you want for it. The arrow keys, Enter and Esc always keep working, whatever you change, so you can never lock yourself out. Reset to defaults puts everything back.

Touch controls and gamepad buttons cannot be remapped.

On a phone​

  • Held upright, the picture sits at the top and a full set of controls sits below it.
  • Held sideways, see-through controls sit in the margins. They lift out of the way when they would cover the text box.
  • The LICK button appears once the blotter is in your fanny pack.
  • Picture size in Options makes the picture smaller if you want more room round it.
