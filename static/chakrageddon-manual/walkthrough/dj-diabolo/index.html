2. DJ Diabolo, the Poi Pit

Goal: beat DJ Diabolo for the Hemp String Wristband. Suggested level: 11–12.

The Poi Pit​

North of the Sandy Trail, the Poi Pit is a real dancefloor: a raised stage across the north with an LED wall, lasers and strobes, poi spinners at both edges, and a packed crowd in front of it. The crowd is an encounter area with a dancer on every tile, and there is no way round it: you have to wade through to reach the steps up onto the stage.

The south end is the chill-out area:

  • A Chai Tent (west) and a stall with the same stock as the Beach Stall. Both stay shut until Diabolo is beaten, so arrive healed.
  • A hippie who explains that Diabolo will not even look at you unless you walk right up on stage and talk to him.
  • A local with the most useful line in the early game: "Every type hits two others hard and bounces off two others. Stimulant flattens Organic and Downer."
  • The Fire Spinner (east) has been spinning fire for six hours without a drink. Bring her two Coconut Waters for A Thirsty Spinner. The Beach Stall sells them for Rs 40.

Trainers​

TrainerWhereAstrals
Raver MikoDancing on the west side of the crowdGlowkid Lv 6, Djembe-Monk Lv 6
Wook SandyDancing on the east side of the crowdWooklet Lv 7

Both dance in place and look around in turns, so with good timing you can slip past. Fighting them is better practice.

Wild Astrals​

  • Day: Djembe-Monk, Glowkid, Shroomba, Wooklet and Ketacrab (Lv 5–7). Blottertot (Lv 6–8) while Peaking.
  • Night: Mozzquito (Lv 5–7), Glowkid (Lv 6–8), Shadowcop (Lv 6–7) and Tridog (Lv 5–7).
  • During Diabolo's headline set (10 PM to 1 AM, once you have the timetable): Ravelord (Lv 20–24).

Items​

  • Tiger Balm: in the east of the chill-out area, near the Fire Spinner.
  • 2 Watermelon Slices (hidden): in the south-east corner. Needs the UV pulse.
  • 3 Rizla Papers: north of the stage, past the fairy lights. You can only get there after you win.

The boss: DJ Diabolo​

Climb the steps in the middle of the stage and talk to him. He has been mixing for nineteen hours and will not be interrupted by anything less than an astral clash.

AstralTypeLevelWeak to
Djembe-MonkStimulant8Dissociative
WookletOrganic / Dissociative8Psychedelic (four times), Organic
GlowkidStimulant / Psychedelic10Bummer

His moment: when his team drops to half its total health he shouts "THIS is the drop!", the music jumps to 176 BPM and his Astral gets faster.

Strategy:

  • A Ketacrab (Dissociative, from the beach or right here in the Pit) handles Djembe-Monk, and Djembe-Monk's Stimulant moves barely scratch it.
  • Blottertot is the hero against Wooklet: Psychedelic moves hit it four times as hard.
  • Glowkid is the real fight. It is the highest level and only Bummer moves hit it hard. A Tridog (Psychedelic / Bummer, from the Sandy Trail) or a night-time Shadowcop can help. Otherwise just bring levels and a few Coconut Waters.
  • If you picked Ganjapod, it shrugs off Wooklet's Dissociative moves, but both Stimulant Astrals hit it hard. Lead with something else.

Reward: Rs 600 and the Hemp String Wristband.

What the wristband unlocks​

Machete Hack: with an Organic or Stimulant Astral in your party, you can now hack through fairy lights and bamboo. The first lights are right behind the stage: walk into them and you are through, on the road north to the Tantric Yurt.

The Chai Tent and the stall open, and the Sandy Trail's ranger lets anyone through from now on.

Rematch

Diabolo offers a rematch once you have three wristbands, but only during his headline set: 10 PM to 1 AM, in the Pit. His team is then Djembe-Monk and Wooklet at Lv 18 and Glowkid at Lv 20. See After the credits.
