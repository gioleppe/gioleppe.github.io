9. The Promoter, the Backstage Container

Goal: beat the Promoter for the All-Access Hologram. Suggested level: 37–38. He brings six Astrals.

Getting there​

Two ways in, both opened by the Ash Mark's Banyan Root Climb:

  • The short way: from the Omelette Stall, climb down the roots at the south exit. You arrive at the Backstage's north door. The Omelette Stall's Chai Tent is the nearest place to heal, just one screen away.
  • The long way: from the Sunset Cliff, climb the roots at the east exit onto the Load-In Road, and follow it east to the Backstage's west door. Worth doing once: it has eight trainers, a side quest and a shop.

The Load-In Road​

The festival's service road, from the Sunset Cliff to the Backstage. A truck park in the north (lorries nose to tail, flight cases, a container stack, light towers), the road itself through the middle with cable ramps and a security checkpoint, the lighting store in the south-west (a maze of flight cases), a fenced generator yard in the south, and a crew catering corner in the south-east, where Chef Bruno sells Masala Chai, Banana Lassi, Watermelon, Coconut Water and Water Bottles.

All three generators are dead, and front-of-house engineer Deepak thinks somebody read a memo about cutting the power: Power Cut. Get them running and the crew throw the Generator Disco in the truck park every night from 9 PM to 3 AM.

The Backstage Container​

"If you can read this you should not be here." The Promoter sits in the big chair at the north of the main hall, behind a velvet rope, with a table of cash and a stack of speakers that shakes.

  • The Backstage Bar (south-east) sells Amber Vials, Rizla Papers, Masala Chai, Watermelon, Electrolyte Sachets, Tiger Balm, Weighted Blankets and Mystery Powder, at "artist prices. You are not an artist, so double." It stays shut until the Promoter is beaten.
  • There is no Chai Tent. The Promoter's place; nobody rests here.
  • A roadie warns you: "The Promoter carries one of everything. No type will save you. Bring a full party and a lot of water."
  • The Green Room, the door in the north wall of the main hall (see below).
  • The velvet rope across the south exit, and a man with a clipboard: "All-Access Hologram or nothing."

The Green Room and Memo 3​

Two of Team Undercover's Bottlers guard the room where the dopamine is bottled:

  • Bottler Kiran: Dengoth Lv 33, Bribe-asaurus Lv 34.
  • Bottler Meera: Selfiend Lv 34, Ravelord Lv 35.

Undercover Memo 3 is in the north-west corner, walled off behind two subwoofers. You need the Bass Drop Shove, and the two must be moved in the right order: if you get it wrong, step out and back in and they reset. 2 Amber Vials wait in the same nook.

Team Undercover's Plan, and Sergeant Baksheesh​

If you have been collecting the memos, this is where the story comes together:

  1. Memo 1: from Undercover Grunt Ravi in the Sunrise Guesthouse (chapter 3). "Phase 1: buy every guesthouse bed in Anjuna. Phase 2: charge for sleep. Phase 3: charge for dreams."
  2. Memo 2: in the Sunset Content Villa on the cliff (chapter 7). "Influencers have fenced off the sunset. Looking west now costs Rs 2000."
  3. Memo 3: here in the Green Room. "When the bottles are full, the Promoter cuts the power to every free party in Goa. Only the Mainstage stays loud. Shred after reading."

Take all three to the hippie at the Flea Market. He pins copies in every Chai Tent from here to Arambol and gives you Rs 2000 and a Mystery Powder. The fence on the cliff comes down, and the sunset is free again: that opens The Freed Sunset, the hunt for Sunraga. Then he warns you: their head of security, Sergeant Baksheesh, has seen the copies and is on his way to the Sunset Cliff.

He waits on the cliff, by the east exit. "I had a permit. I wrote it myself."

The boss: The Promoter​

"Seven wristbands. Barefoot. No passport..."

His moment: "Security! Nobody touches the talent." His Astral's Ego defence goes up two stages, so physical moves do much less from then on.

Strategy:

  • The roadie is right: one of everything. Bring six Astrals and a spread of types.
  • A Dissociative Astral handles Bribe-asaurus and Amphetadragon (four times). Hoffmanite, Grand Wook, Neighbula or Wonkrab.
  • A Bummer Astral handles Ravelord and Omeletron (four times).
  • A Psychedelic or Organic one for Wonkrab, and a Stimulant or Organic one for Duvetghast.
  • After his moment, switch to astral moves (the ones that use Fractal power), which his raised Ego defence does not touch.
  • Amphetadragon, his last and strongest, has Overdrive: below a third of its health its Stimulant moves hit 30% harder. Finish it quickly.

Reward: Rs 5000 and the All-Access Hologram. "The Mainstage Temple will let you in."

What the wristband unlocks​

The man with the clipboard steps aside, and the velvet rope at the south exit opens onto the Last Dancefloor. The Backstage Bar opens too, which is the best shop for the end of the game.

Rematch

The Promoter only offers a rematch after the credits: everything ten levels higher (Lv 45–47).
