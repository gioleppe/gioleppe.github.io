6. Baron Von Darkpsy, the 190 BPM Forest Stage

Goal: beat Baron Von Darkpsy for the UV Skeleton Band. Suggested level: 26–27.

This is the biggest stretch of the map so far, with two optional areas (the Sea Grotto and Fishermen's Cove) and three side quests. Keep a Dissociative Astral in your party for floating, and a Psychedelic one for the dark Forest Stage.

Getting there​

Two routes, and both are worth walking:

  • By sea: from the Scooter Ditch, float north onto the Arabian Sea Shallows, cross the bay to its east edge, go east to Arambol Sweetwater Lake, then south to the Forest Stage.
  • Through the bamboo: from the Omelette Stall, hack north into the Dense Bamboo Thicket, float across its channel, and go north to the Forest Stage.

The Arabian Sea Shallows​

A wide bay, mostly water. A long sunbathing beach runs down the west side, with sandbars and a sandy spit in the east, a rocky islet in the middle with the Sea Grotto inside it, and the Sadhu Cave's mouth up in the north cliffs (a sadhu with a staff turns you away until Chloe is beaten). East is the lake, south the Scooter Ditch.

On the beach, Sharon from Basildon and her party have used cooking oil as sun cream ("factor nothing") and are going red: Mad Dogs and Englishmen.

caution

Finishing Mad Dogs and Englishmen turns the four tourists blue and happy, and they stop fighting. If you want to battle Nigel, Tracy and Dave, do it before you bring Sharon the clay.

The Sea Grotto​

Float to the islet in the middle and go in through the gap on its south landing. Inside it is dark, with tide pools.

  • Smuggler Vasco: Mozzquito Lv 23, Lobsterlad Lv 24.
  • Cave Diver Meera: Neighbula Lv 23, Nitroxide Lv 24.
  • Cool Blue Clay at the very back, in the north-east: the thing Sharon needs. "THE CLAY IS AT THE BACK", as somebody scratched on the sign.
  • Amber Vial (hidden) in the south-west corner.
  • Wild: Ketacrab (Lv 21–24), Lobsterlad (Lv 22–25), Neighbula (Lv 21–24), Sirenowl (Lv 22–24).

Arambol Sweetwater Lake​

A lake with a small island, a jetty and woods round the edge. "Things swim in the lake that do not swim anywhere else."

Wild in the water: Neighbula and Lassiblob (Lv 22–24), Wonkrab (Lv 24–25). At night with your ego gone, Lucidra (Lv 27–30).

The Dense Bamboo Thicket​

North of the Omelette Stall. Fairy lights block the way in from the south and again further on, and a channel of water cuts the thicket in two.

  • Wook Moss: Wooklet Lv 21, Chillem Lv 22.
  • Fire-Spinner Ash: Glowkid Lv 22, Djembe-Monk Lv 22, Tridog Lv 22.
  • Mantequilla is crouched in a gap at the east edge, picking mushrooms and humming. He drove from Vicchio, in Tuscany, in a Fiat Panda, following the aliens. Talk to him: The Aliens of Vicchio.

Items: a Record Crate on the west side, a Warm Kingfisher in the north-east, 2 Electrolyte Sachets in the south-west and 1 Electrolyte Sachet (hidden) in the south-east.

Wild: Wooklet, Glowkid, Shroomba, Chillem and Mozzquito (Lv 20–23), and rarely Grand Wook (Lv 29). In the water: Ketacrab and Neighbula (Lv 20–23).

The 190 BPM Forest Stage​

A clearing in the jungle with a dancefloor, UV light, fog and glowing mushrooms. The trees pulse to the beat. It is dark: you need the UV pulse to see more than a few tiles.

  • A Chai Tent in the south-west, shut until the Baron is beaten.
  • A wook (west) with the hint you want: "His Astrals are Bummer and Psychedelic. Downers put both to sleep. I would know. I am one."
  • The DJ Booth, the door in the north-east hut: Selector Nandini (Sirenowl Lv 26, Glowkid Lv 26, Dengoth Lv 27), a pickup of 2 Electrolyte Sachets, and a roadie whose record crates were dumped around the jungle (Lost Crates).

The boss: Baron Von Darkpsy​

He conducts the fog from the stage, facing away from the crowd. Walk up and talk to him.

His moment: "One-ninety was the warm-up. Welcome to two hundred." His Astral gets two stages faster.

Strategy:

  • Downer moves hit Sirenowl, Fractoad and Omnihound hard (Omnihound four times). Duvetghast, Snoozel, Lassiblob or Chillem.
  • Dissociative moves flatten Dengoth and Sirenowl.
  • Neighbula (Dissociative / Downer) does both jobs at once and takes almost nothing from his Bummer moves. If you caught one at the Ditch, the Shallows or the lake, this is its fight.
  • Psychedelic, Stimulant and Organic moves are resisted by three of his four. Leave them on the bench.
  • After his moment his Astral will usually move first. Keep your healing items for then.

Reward: Rs 2600 and the UV Skeleton Band.

What the wristband unlocks​

Jaw Grind: with a Stimulant Astral in your party, you can chew through padlocked VIP fences. The ones that matter are at the Flea Market's south exit, which leads to the Dry Ice Corridor and the Sunset Cliff.

Rematch

The Baron offers a rematch once you have seven wristbands: Sirenowl Lv 33, Dengoth and Fractoad Lv 34, Omnihound Lv 37.

Optional: Fishermen's Cove​

With four wristbands, the lifeguard at the east end of Driftwood Beach lets you round the rocks. Fishermen's Cove is a big, quiet area between the beach and the Sunset Cliff, and a good place to level up at this point (trainers Lv 17–21).

  • The west: the Scramble, rock pools and dune grass.
  • The north: a cliff-foot path under the red rock. Banyan roots at the far north-east climb up to the Sunset Cliff, but you need the Ash Mark for them (chapter 8). Coming down from the cliff later is free.
  • The middle: a fishing village. Boats pulled up on the sand, nets drying, Rosa's hut and her grandfather Santan in his hammock, a fish stall, a little sea shrine, and the toddy shack, whose seller has a shop (Coconut Water, Watermelon, Water Bottles, Electrolyte Sachets).
  • The east: a palm grove.
  • The south: the sea, with two islets.

The tide​

The flats between the shore and the islets are wet sand from 4 to 10, morning and evening, and surf the rest of the time. If the tide turns while you are out there, you splash back to the nearest dry sand. A tide table is painted on a sign, and flat rocks (one by the sign on the shore, and one out by each islet) let you sit and wait for the tide to go out.

Rosa tells you that something came out of the sea and dragged her grandfather's boat, the Maria da Graça, into the cave on the islet: Low Tide. The Sea Cave is only reachable across the flats at low tide.

The Sea Cave​

Across the flats, in the islet's cliff. Inside: the Maria da Graça wedged between the rocks with something enormous snoring in her, a Mystery Powder on the east side, a Masala Chai in the west, and wild Wonkrab and Lobsterlad (Lv 21–24), Sirenowl and Duvetghast (Lv 21–23), and Lobsterlad in the water.
