4. Captain Keta, the Sunken Scooter Ditch

Goal: beat Captain Keta for the Wonky Keychain. Suggested level: 18–19.

Getting there​

From the Flea Market, go north past the security guard onto the Clogged Dirt Road. Keep a Psychedelic Astral in your party: the road and the Jungle Shortcut are dark, and the UV pulse is what lights them.

Halfway up the road a sacred cow called Lakshmi has been lying across it since Tuesday, and nobody is going to argue. You cannot get past her. Instead, take one of the two detours. Both leave the road near its south end and come back onto it near the north end, past the cow:

  • West: the Jungle Shortcut. "It is not shorter." Longer, wilder, more trainers, and the lost tour group.
  • East: the Roadside Shrine. A gate, a long walk, a marigold garden and a courtyard. The shrine's people have three side quests between them.

Either way, at the top of the road go north into the Scooter Ditch.

The Clogged Dirt Road​

A scooter jam in the dark, round a cow.

  • Raver Jun at the north end: Glowkid Lv 15, Djembe-Monk Lv 15.
  • Amber Vial (hidden): near the shrine turn-off in the south-east.
  • Wild: Shadowcop, Mozzquito, Wooklet and Ketacrab (Lv 12–15), Sirenowl (Lv 13–15).

Later, A Coconut for Lakshmi gets the cow off the road for good.

The Jungle Shortcut (west)​

Five bends of dark jungle, climbing north. A stream with a single plank crosses it about halfway up, and Wook Basil watches the plank.

At the bottom, Tour Leader Fergus of Gap Year Adventures has lost three backpackers. Beat each of them as you go and they walk back to him: The Lost Tour Group.

The Roadside Shrine (east)​

Enter at the gate in the south. Guard Pinto and Flower Seller Meena stand at the gate, Baba Neem on the long walk north, then you reach the marigold garden, climb the steps into the courtyard with the shrine, and leave by the north side back onto the road.

In the courtyard:

  • Pujari Mohan explains that Lakshmi is not stubborn, she is offended: A Coconut for Lakshmi.
  • The old woman finds fresh marigolds on the shrine every morning and has stopped asking where they come from: Flowers Before Dawn.

Items: a Masala Chai in the west of the courtyard, an Amber Vial (hidden) in the marigold garden, and 2 Incense Sticks in the clearing north of the courtyard.

Wild: Djembe-Monk, Snoozel, Lassiblob and Shadowcop (Lv 12–15), Chillem (Lv 13–15), Sirenowl (Lv 13–16).

The Sunken Scooter Ditch​

A ditch full of half-sunk scooters with their indicators still blinking, and a stage in the middle with a captain's chair on it.

  • A Chai Tent in the south-west, shut until Keta is beaten.
  • A wook in the north-east knows the way on: "The sea goes to the lake, and the lake goes to the forest stage. You would need to float though." The north edge of the ditch is water.

Wild: Ketacrab, Wooklet, Crabcake and Lassiblob (Lv 13–16), Nitroxide (Lv 14–16), Neighbula (Lv 15–16). Once you can float, the water holds Ketacrab (Lv 16–20) and Neighbula (Lv 18–20).

tip

Catch a Neighbula here if you can. It is Dissociative / Downer, takes almost nothing from Bummer moves, and will be exactly what you want against Baron Von Darkpsy. It is also a Dissociative, which you need for the Pink Flamingo Float.

The boss: Captain Keta​

"Ahoy... is the ditch moving, or is it me? It is me. I am the ditch."

His moment: "All hands! Sound the horns!" Scooter horns blare and the battle gets more intoxicated, which means more critical hits for both sides.

Strategy:

  • Psychedelic moves hit every one of his Astrals hard. Blottertot or Fractoad is ideal; a Glowkid or Shroomba works too.
  • Organic moves are the other answer to the crabs.
  • Do not bother with Stimulant or Bummer moves: Dissociative Astrals shrug them off.
  • Nitroxide has the Burst ability: its first move after coming out hits twice as hard, and it pops at the end of its second turn whatever you do. Put something sturdy in front of it, or knock it out first.
  • Keta's crabs have Dreamer: one attack in seven simply drifts past them. That is not bad luck, it is the ability.

Reward: Rs 1500 and the Wonky Keychain.

What the wristband unlocks​

Bass Drop Shove: with a Downer or Organic Astral in your party, you can shove subwoofers and passed-out wooks one tile. The one that matters is in the Flea Market's east gap: shove it and the way to the Back Alley and the Omelette Stall is open. Pushed things roll back when you leave, so you can never jam a gap for good.

Three wristbands also means the festival timetable is on its way (next chapter).

Rematch

Keta offers a rematch once you have five wristbands: Ketacrab and Wooklet Lv 26, Nitroxide Lv 25, Wonkrab Lv 28.
