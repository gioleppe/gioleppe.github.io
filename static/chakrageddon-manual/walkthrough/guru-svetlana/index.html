3. Guru Svetlana, the Tantric Yurt

Goal: beat Guru Svetlana for the Quartz Wristband. Suggested level: 15–16.

Getting there​

Hack through the fairy lights behind the Poi Pit's stage (Machete Hack) and walk north. You come out at the south end of the Tantric Yurt's grounds. The yurt itself is at the north end, with Svetlana on her cushion inside. The east exit leads to the Anjuna Flea Market, which you can visit straight away (see below).

The Tantric Yurt​

"Shoes off. Ego off."

  • A Chai Tent in the south-east, shut until Svetlana is beaten.
  • A tourist who paid for a ten-day retreat and was told on day two that he was already enlightened.
  • An acolyte inside the yurt, and, during the morning Ecstatic dance (8 to 10 AM, once you have the timetable), a gong player.

Wild Astrals​

  • Day: Tridog (Lv 8–11), Shroomba (Lv 8–11), Glowkid (Lv 9–11), Lassiblob (Lv 9–11), Snoozel (Lv 8–10). Fractoad (Lv 10–12) while Peaking.
  • Night: Mozzquito (Lv 9–11), Shadowcop (Lv 9–11), Tridog (Lv 9–12) and Sirenowl (Lv 11–12).
  • During the Ecstatic dance (8 to 10 AM): Chillem (Lv 20–24).

Items​

  • 2 Watermelon Slices: on the west side, by a lamp.
  • Banana Lassi: in the north-east corner, beside the yurt. It is a catalyst: give it to a Snoozel and it becomes a Duvetghast on the spot.

Next door: the Anjuna Flea Market​

The market east of the Yurt is the first hub and the best shop in the early game. It is worth a visit before the boss.

  • The market vendor (north-west) sells everything a traveller needs: Ziploc Baggies and Rizla Papers, Coconut Water, Watermelon, Water Bottles, Electrolyte Sachets, Tiger Balm, Incense Sticks, Wraparound Shades, Earplugs, two catalysts (Warm Kingfisher, Banana Lassi), all four held items (Friendship Band, Neon Glowstick, Lucky Rupee, Steel Flask) and two outfits (Sequin Vest, Glow Jacket).
  • The Sunrise Guesthouse (the door in the north-west): a bed for Rs 100, the tourist who lost the sandals you may already be carrying (The Lost Sandals), and Undercover Grunt Ravi (Shadowcop Lv 14, Mozzquito Lv 14). Beat him and he drops Undercover Memo 1.
  • The market hippie tells you about Team Undercover, and asks you to bring him any of their paperwork. This starts Team Undercover's Plan.
  • The collector (east) has never seen a proper Downer up close: A Collector's Eye. A Snoozel in your party is enough.
  • Dealer Vik (south-east), a trainer: Shadowcop Lv 13, Mozzquito Lv 13.
  • The Lassi Wallah explains catalysts: "Lassi for the sleepy ones, Kingfisher for the sunburned, powder for the glowing."
  • A tuk-tuk driver who only takes press. Remember him.

The market's other exits are all blocked for now: north by security ("Guru Svetlana decides who passes"), east by a subwoofer in the gap (Captain Keta's wristband), and south by padlocked VIP fences (Baron Von Darkpsy's).

Market items: 2 Incense Sticks in the east, an Electrolyte Sachet in the west, and 2 Masala Chai (hidden) in the north-east, which need the UV pulse you are about to earn.

Market wild Astrals: Lassiblob (Lv 11–14), Tridog (Lv 11–14), Djembe-Monk (Lv 11–14), Snoozel (Lv 11–13), Chillem (Lv 12–14) and Selfiend (Lv 13–14) by day; Shadowcop, Mozzquito, Sirenowl and Glowkid (around Lv 11–14) at night.

The boss: Guru Svetlana​

AstralTypeLevelWeak to
BlottertotPsychedelic12Downer, Bummer

FractoadPsychedelic14Downer, Bummer

Her moment: at half health she chants, and the type chart turns upside down for three turns: "Now your strengths are your weaknesses, darling."

Strategy:

  • Bring Downers. A Snoozel (beach, trail, the Yurt itself) or a Lassiblob (the Yurt and the market) hits all three hard, and Psychedelic moves bounce off them.
  • Bummer moves are the backup: Tridog, Shadowcop (at night) or Crabcake.
  • When the chart flips, your Downer moves suddenly do a quarter or half damage. For those three turns, switch tactics: Dissociative moves now hit her Blottertot and Fractoad hard, and Stimulant or Organic moves hit Tridog hard. Or simply heal and wait the three turns out.

Reward: Rs 1000 and the Quartz Wristband.

What the wristband unlocks​

Blacklight UV Pulse: with a Psychedelic Astral in your party, dark places are lit up and hidden items appear. Go back for the Water Bottle in the Sandy Trail's tide pools, the 2 Watermelon Slices in the Poi Pit and the Masala Chai in the market.

The market's security guard now lets you north: "The Guru vouched for you. Bring a light, it is black as a speaker cone up there."

Rematch

Svetlana offers a rematch once you have four wristbands: Blottertot and Tridog at Lv 22, Fractoad at Lv 24.
