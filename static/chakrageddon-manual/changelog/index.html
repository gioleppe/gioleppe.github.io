Changelog

Every version of Chakra-geddon since the first release, newest first. The game updates itself in your browser, so the version on the title screen is the one described at the top of this page.

Highlights

  • 1.0.0: the first release: eight Wristbands, 36 Astrals, the Mainstage Temple and the VIP Shaman Council, playable on phone and PC and installable for offline play.
  • 1.0.4 to 1.0.20: a new opening dream, intoxication with real stakes, a goal and route hints, interiors, side quests and the Quests tab, and a drawn festival map with tuk-tuk travel.
  • 1.0.41: a pixel cutscene for every boss, the Four, the rival and the Council.
  • 1.0.46: being high changes the world: hallucinations, places that exist only with your ego gone, and two new Astrals.
  • 1.0.47 and 1.0.48: the festival timetable, and boss fights with arrivals, half-health moments, their own stages and a wristband ceremony.
  • 1.1.0: music that behaves, and sleeping until the set you want.
  • 1.2.0: Astrals grow by chance, moves cost more water, and a Difficulty setting.
  • 1.2.3: The Unfinished Mandala, a quest for Mandalord.
  • 1.2.5: three new routes (Fishermen's Cove, the Load-In Road and the Last Dancefloor) and the Mainstage Temple rebuilt as a ziggurat.
  • 1.3.0: the manual and walkthrough go online, with a round of fixes it turned up.
  • 1.4.0: the whole game in Italian, with a language switch on the title and in Options.
  • 1.4.1: menus that wrap round, Astrals that come in and go back in battle, and incense you wade through.

1.4.1 — 2026-11-14​

New

  • Menus wrap round. Press down on the last row to come back to the top, up on the first to reach the bottom; in the battle menu and the move list, left and right wrap round too.
  • Astrals come in and go back. Yours and every trainer's arrive as a point of light that bursts open, the creature growing out of it; a wild one shimmers out of the haze. Swapped out, an Astral turns to light and streaks back to its owner before the next one comes in.
  • Wading through incense. Clouds of incense billow when you step into them and wrap round your feet while you stand in them, like tall grass; dark bushes shake. With Reduced motion on, nothing moves.

1.4.0 — 2026-11-14​

New

  • Italiano. The whole game is translated: dialogue, menus, battles, quests, names and the credits. Pick the language with Language on the title screen or at the top of Options; the game uses your browser's language the first time. The page restarts when you switch (your progress is saved first).
  • The game can take more languages: they are plain dictionary files, one per language.

1.3.0 — 2026-11-14​

New

  • The Chakra-geddon manual: a user manual, a full walkthrough, reference pages and this changelog, online at gioleppe.github.io/chakrageddon-manual.

Changed

  • Sergeant Baksheesh brings a stronger team (Lv 33 to 35, five Astrals). He only turns up after the third memo, which is behind Baba Smoke, and his old team was far too weak by then. He pays more, too.
  • Dreamer now dodges exactly what it says: one attack in seven (it was a little more).

Fixed

  • The hidden Masala Chai on the Last Dancefloor was under Darkpsy Superfan Vlad, where nobody could reach it. It is in the clearing beside him now.
  • The Mainstage Temple's sign, the Water Auntie, Chai Mama, Lead Lakshmi and the Council talked about doors and floors; since the ziggurat they talk about terraces and stairs.
  • In the Blottertot version of the last rival fight, his ace was Lv 40 instead of Lv 43 like the other two.
  • The tuk-tuk driver at the Flea Market now tells you to ride from the Map, where the tuk-tuk actually is.
  • Banyan's tip no longer says chai brings you down: water, a nap in a Chai Tent or a long walk do.

1.2.5 — 2026-11-14​

New

  • Fishermen's Cove, a large new area between the beach and the Sunset Cliff: rock pools, a fishing village with a toddy shack and its own shop, a palm grove and two islets. A lifeguard lets you round the rocks once you have four Wristbands.
  • Tides: in the cove the flats are wet sand from 4 to 10, morning and evening, and sea the rest of the time. Get caught as the tide turns and you splash back to dry sand; sit on a flat rock to wait for it. A tide table is posted on a sign.
  • Low Tide quest: cross the flats to a sea cave and find Rosa's grandfather's boat, with a fridge-sized Lobsterlad asleep in it. Afterwards Rosa rows you up the coast to the Arabian Sea Shallows and back.
  • The Load-In Road, the festival's service road between the Sunset Cliff and the Backstage Container: a truck park, a security checkpoint, a lighting store maze, a generator yard and a crew catering corner with a shop.
  • Power Cut quest: get all three generators running again (a pulled cable, an empty tank and a Bottler "doing maintenance"), for a reward that includes Earplugs.
  • The Generator Disco, an eighth set on the timetable: 21:00 to 03:00 in the truck park once the power is back, with festoon lights, lasers, a DJ and a dancing Glowkid.
  • The Last Dancefloor, a victory road south of the Backstage: lanes of dancers that move along with every step you take, a superfan of every boss, the Front Row, wild Astrals in the packed crowd and a Chai Tent in the welfare tent.
  • At the front of the Last Dancefloor the crowd lifts you, passes you overhead and throws you onto the stage, where the temple is revealed. The second time you get a short version.
  • Lost in the Crowd quest: help Ziggy find Noor, who promised not to move from the light towers.
  • The Mainstage Temple is now a terraced ziggurat, five terraces climbing into the night, each with its own look, guarded by one of the Four in order and then the rival. Nobody can be walked round.
  • A cutscene when you first enter the temple: the terraces light up one by one with the music and the Council look down at you.
  • Eight new trainers in the cove, eight on the Load-In Road and ten on the Last Dancefloor, plus new pickups in each.

Changed

  • The path down from the Sunset Cliff now comes out in Fishermen's Cove; climbing back up still needs the Banyan Root Climb.
  • The Sunset Cliff's east exit and the Backstage's west exit now lead onto the Load-In Road.
  • The back stairs at the foot of the temple bring you out at the side gate of the Last Dancefloor.

  • Battles come alive: effects for every move type, trainer portraits, backdrops for each kind of place by day and night, and proper back views of your Astrals.

Changed

  • The Clogged Dirt Road, the Sunken Scooter Ditch and the Poi Pit are reshaped, with hidden pickups.

New

  • People who wander around.
  • Side quests: the tourist's lost sandals, and exposing Team Undercover with the three memos.
  • Banyan rewards a fuller Trip Journal.
  • Every boss offers one stronger rematch two Wristbands later.

1.0.7 — 2026-11-12​

New

  • Five buildings to walk into: Banyan's Shack, the Sunrise Guesthouse, the DJ Booth, the Sunset Content Villa and the Green Room, with a subwoofer puzzle and three Team Undercover memos to find.

1.0.6 — 2026-11-12​

New

  • A current goal and route hints: entering an area tells you the way, and new goals are announced.
  • A Map tab in the Fanny Pack.

1.0.5 — 2026-11-12​

New

  • Being high now matters: four levels of high bring more Vibes, better catches and rare Astrals, but also tougher wild Astrals, bags that fly off and bigger losses on a whiteout. Some people say different things, and two only appear while you are Peaking.
  • The Glowing Blotter can be used from the Stash Tin, and water always brings you down.

1.0.4 — 2026-11-12​

New

  • A new opening: Banyan explains the world while you lie passed out on the beach. You choose your name, and he uses it.
  • Portraits for every character.

Behind the scenes: an automatic test suite that plays and checks the whole game.

Fixed

  • Text that overlapped or ran off the screen in the shop, the Journal and the battle party list.

1.0.2 — 2026-11-12​

Fixed

  • Move names no longer overlap in the battle, learn-move and summary screens.

1.0.1 — 2026-11-12​

Fixed

  • Pressing A through Banyan's lines no longer picks a starter for you. The starter choice now shows each creature's card, and "Sure?" starts on "Let me think".

1.0.0 — 2026-11-12​

New

  • The first release: the beach and the whole festival, eight bosses and their Wristbands, 36 Astrals to bag, Team Undercover, the Mainstage Temple with the Four and the VIP Shaman Council, an ending and an open festival afterwards.
  • Plays in the browser on phones and PCs, with touch, keyboard and gamepad controls and key remapping.
  • Installable as an app that works offline, with saves you can export and import.
  • A content notice on first launch, with grounded mode offered for gentler effects.
