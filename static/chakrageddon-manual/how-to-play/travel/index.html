Travel

The festival stretches along the coast from Anjuna to Arambol: beaches, stages, jungle shortcuts, a flea market, a sacred cow in the middle of the road, and eventually a temple. Most of it you cover on foot, which is fine, because nobody here is going anywhere in particular anyway.

Walking and running​

  • Walk with the d-pad. Walk off the edge of a map, or through a door, to reach the next area.
  • Run by holding B. You move twice as fast. "Everyone at this festival is in a hurry. Nobody knows where to." You cannot run while floating.
  • Encounter tiles (incense clouds, dark bush, packed dancefloors, water if you are floating) bring wild Astrals. Light an Incense Stick to keep them away for 120 steps.
  • Trainers watch in a straight line. Step into it and the fight is on. Sometimes you can sneak round; often you cannot.
  • Some ways are shut until you have the right wristband and the right Astral with you (see Wristbands and Techniques), or until somebody lets you through. Bump into what blocks you and it tells you why.

Lost? The Map tab in the Fanny Pack shows your goal and the way to go next. Stuck in a corner? System › Unstuck puts you back where you came into the area.

The festival clock​

The festival never stops, but the clock does move. It is shown on the Sets page once you have the timetable.

  • A new trip starts at 4:00 PM.
  • The clock only moves when you walk. One festival minute passes every seven steps, so an hour goes by in a bit over a minute of walking. Standing still, talking, shopping and fighting take no time at all.
  • Sleeping at a Chai Tent until a set, waiting for the tide, and whiting out (six hours) all move the clock forward.

Day and night​

It is night from 7 PM to 6 AM. The light fades slowly over a couple of hours at dusk and dawn, and when the day turns a band of sky sweeps across the top of the screen: a moon and "Night falls", or a sun and "The sun comes up".

At night:

  • the Flea Market, the Omelette Stall, the Poi Pit, the Sandy Trail and the Tantric Yurt have different wild Astrals;
  • some Astrals and some people only come out after dark;
  • battles get a night backdrop.

Several things happen only at certain hours: the sets on the Festival Timetable, the tides at Fishermen's Cove, a sunrise errand, a sunset legend. People will mention them.

The tuk-tuk​

Beat Influencer Chloe and her VIP Press Pass gets you the Share Taxi Tuk-Tuk. "Tuk-tuk drivers salute it."

  1. Open the Fanny Pack and go to the Map tab.
  2. Move the cursor to a place with a tent marker: somewhere you have rested at a Chai Tent.
  3. The label says A: tuk-tuk. Press A.

A yellow and green tuk-tuk bounces you down the night road, horn first, and drops you at that tent.

  • Every Chai Tent you rest at becomes a stop. So rest at every tent you pass.
  • A few places are out of a tuk-tuk's reach: you cannot call one from deep inside the temple, a sea cave or a room that exists only in your head. Walk out first.

Fishermen's Cove and Rosa's boat​

At the east end of Driftwood Shack Beach, a lifeguard turns you back from the rocks until you have four wristbands. Beyond them lies Fishermen's Cove: a fishing village with boats pulled up on the sand, nets drying, a toddy shack with its own shop, and the sea with two islets. From the cove, a cliff path leads to the Sunset Cliff. Coming down it is free; going up needs the Banyan Root Climb.

The tides​

The flats between the shore and the islets are only dry at low tide. The painted tide table on the sign says it all:

Low tideHigh tide
4 AM to 10 AM10 AM to 4 PM
4 PM to 10 PM10 PM to 4 AM
  • At low tide the flats are wet sand you can walk across. Some things out there can only be reached then.
  • At high tide the surf covers them and nobody can cross. "The sea does not care about your plans."
  • If the tide turns while you are out on the flats, you splash back to the nearest dry sand.
  • Flat rocks let you sit and wait for the tide to go out. "It thinks about it for a long time, and then it goes."

Rosa's boat​

Rosa in the village has a problem with her grandfather's boat (see the quest Low Tide). Help her, and from then on Rosa rows you up the coast to the Arabian Sea Shallows and back, whenever you like. Talk to her on either beach. "It is quicker than walking, and the dolphins are free."

The Last Dancefloor​

Beyond the velvet rope behind the Backstage Container lies the festival's last and biggest dancefloor, the road to the Mainstage Temple. It is packed.

  • Dancing lanes: on many rows the crowd dances sideways, one tile with every step you take, in groups with gaps between them. Some rows move faster, some slower, some the other way.
  • Nobody dances onto you, and walking into a dancer simply makes you wait a beat for a gap. You can never be boxed in. Watch the rhythm and step through.
  • Superfans of every boss wait along the way, and wild Astrals live in the thickest crowds. The welfare tent near the start has a Chai Tent and a merch stall.
  • At the very front, the crowd might just give you a lift. They are picky about who they lift.
