Chai Tents and Whiteouts

Chai Tents​

The striped Chai Tents are your safe havens. Inside each one, Chai Mama (a tired woman in a crochet top who has seen everything, twice) looks after anyone who staggers in. Walk up to a tent and press A.

She asks: Rest, Check the stash or Leave.

Rest​

"Here is watermelon and a heavy blanket." A rest:

  • restores all Serotonin and all Hydration for your whole party, and for every Astral in the stash;
  • cures every status condition, and revives fainted Astrals;
  • brings you all the way down to a Microdose;
  • makes this tent your whiteout point: where you wake up if you lose;
  • adds this tent to your tuk-tuk stops, once you can ride (see Travel).

It costs nothing. "Do not tell me what you saw."

Once you have the Festival Timetable, Chai Mama also offers to let you sleep until a set starts. Pick which set from the timetable, soonest first, and the night goes by on a clock in the dark. If the set is right there at that tent, you get a look at the crowd and the stage as you wake. See The Festival Timetable.

The stash​

You carry up to six Astrals. Everyone else waits in the stash, which Chai Mama keeps at every tent. When your party is full, newly bagged Astrals go straight there.

Choose Check the stash and your party page opens with the stash unlocked. From there you can:

  • Take an Astral from the stash into your party (if you have room);
  • Swap with... a party Astral, in one go;
  • Send to stash one of your party (you must keep at least one);
  • read anyone's Summary.

Sort the stash by order caught, name or level with the chip at the top (or left and right).

Away from a tent you can still look at who is in the stash, but not swap. "Swap at a Chai Tent: Chai Mama keeps the stash." Checking the stash also makes the tent your whiteout point.

Other places to rest​

  • The Sunrise Guesthouse at the Flea Market has a bed for Rs 100 a nap.
  • Banyan's hammock, in his shack on Driftwood Shack Beach, is free. "It smells of incense and 1994."

Both work like a Chai Tent's rest, without the stash.

Tents near a boss​

Some Chai Tents sit right next to a boss's stage, and Chai Mama there will not patch you up for another go at them: "Not while DJ Diabolo is still on, beta. Everyone who walks in mid-set wants a blanket and a second go." Until that boss is beaten, the tent:

  • does not heal,
  • is not a whiteout point,
  • and is not a tuk-tuk stop.

The shops in those areas keep their shutters down too. So heal up before you walk onto a boss's turf, and bring snacks.

The Mainstage Temple's tent is a special case. It works until you start your climb against the Four-on-the-Floor. Once you have beaten one of them, Chai Mama will not let you sit until you have beaten all four: "You started. Finish, then sit."

Whiteouts​

When your last Astral fades out, you do not black out. You white out: "You have nobody left standing. Everything goes white..."

Then:

  1. You lose some rupees. 15% of what you carry, or more if you were high: 22.5% when Peaking, 30% at Ego gone.
  2. One random outfit you own walks off with somebody. (If you own none, you lose none.)
  3. Six hours pass.
  4. You wake at your last resting place, with your whole party and stash fully healed, stone sober. "Somebody has tucked you in."

You keep your Astrals, their levels and Vibes, your items (apart from that outfit), your wristbands and your quest progress.

Lost an outfit? A shop outfit is back in stock, so you can buy it again. For a gift outfit, go back to whoever gave it to you: they always seem to have a spare.

Losing to a boss​

The boss stays where they are, at full strength, and you can try again whenever you like. Their cutscene plays again in full. The nearby tent is still shut, so your whiteout point is wherever you rested before.

Losing halfway up the Mainstage Temple sends the Four back to their posts: the climb starts over from the bottom.

Your reflection​

At Ego gone you may end up fighting your own reflection. Losing to it is not a whiteout. "Your reflection wins. It does not seem to mind." Everything is put back the way it was.

tip

A whiteout costs a share of the rupees you carry, so the more you carry, the more you lose. Before a risky fight, spend your rupees on things you will use anyway (bags, water, a Weighted Blanket), and come down before a boss if you can: it halves the bill compared with Ego gone.
