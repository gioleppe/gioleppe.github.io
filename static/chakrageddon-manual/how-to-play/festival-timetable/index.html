The Festival Timetable

It is an infinite seven-day festival, so something is always playing somewhere. Once you know the timetable, you can be in the right place at the right time.

Getting the timetable​

After your third wristband, the next time you arrive somewhere, Prof. Banyan jogs up beside you with his photocopy of the Festival Timetable, folded into eighths. It adds a Sets tab to the Fanny Pack and pink markers on the map.

Before that, the stages are just stages: no sets play.

The Sets page​

The Sets tab shows the festival clock and every set in order of start time.

  • The set playing now is marked LIVE.
  • A set starting within two hours says how long until it begins ("in 40m", "in 1h").
  • The set under the cursor shows where it plays, until when, and what it is like.

On the region map, a pink marker shows where a set is on right now.

The Generator Disco​

The Generator Disco is not on the timetable at first. The Load-In Road's three generators have all died at once, and somebody read a memo about cutting the power. Get all three running again (the quest Power Cut) and the crew throw their own party every night from 21:00. It then appears on the Sets page like any other set.

During a set​

  • The area fills with a crowd. On the big dancefloors every tile is packed.
  • The music switches to the set's own mood and tempo, and "Live now" appears as you arrive.
  • Someone special turns up for the occasion.
  • A set-only Astral comes out of the encounter tiles. These Astrals appear nowhere else in the wild, and they come out during their set whether it is day or night.

Some quests and some people also care about which set is on. The Journal's Where page lists a set-only Astral's set once you have seen it.

Sleeping until a set​

You do not have to walk the clock round. Once you have the timetable, Chai Mama offers you more after a rest:

  1. Rest at any Chai Tent.
  2. When she asks "...Sleep here until a set starts?", choose Sleep until a set.
  3. Pick the set you want from the list, soonest first. A set at this very tent is marked "here".

The night goes by on a clock in the dark, and you wake as the set begins. If it is right where you are sleeping, the camera goes over to the crowd and the stage before Chai Mama speaks.

tip

Combine this with the tuk-tuk: rest at the tent nearest the set you want, sleep until it starts, and walk straight into the crowd. For the Darkpsy hour, rest at the Forest Stage's tent; for Diabolo's headline set, the Poi Pit's.
