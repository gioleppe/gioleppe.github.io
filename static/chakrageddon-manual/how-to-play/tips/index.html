Tips for New Players

A satire. Hydrate. Look after each other. And also:

Your first hour​

  • Talk to everybody. People hint at hidden Astrals, hand out quests, and occasionally give you things for free. A "!" over someone's head means they have a quest for you.
  • Bag a second Astral early. The ranger at the top of the Sandy Trail will not let you through with just one, and a second Astral is a lifesaver against the first trainers anyway. Incense clouds are where the wild ones drift out.
  • Read the summary. In the Fanny Pack, open an Astral's summary, point at a move and press A. It tells you exactly what the move does. Same for stats, types and abilities.
  • Use Quick Items. Q, ITEMS or X on a gamepad heals your party from the map without digging through menus.

Water is everything​

  • Every move costs Hydration. Strong moves cost a lot. An Astral that runs dry can only Dry Heave, and that hurts it too.
  • Carry Water Bottles. They are cheap (Rs 50), they refill 40 Hydration, and the sip you take brings you down a band. Electrolyte Sachets refill an Astral completely.
  • Mix cheap and strong moves. A cheap move that does the job is often better than a big one that leaves you parched three fights later.
  • Trainers do not drink in battle. You can. That is a real advantage in a long fight.

Battles​

  • Play the type chart. Doubled damage is a big deal, and a matching type adds another 1.5 times on top. The move panel says "resonates" once you have seen the foe before.
  • Switch out of a bad matchup. Switching costs a turn, but it also clears a Thought Loop and any lowered stats.
  • Use the free swap. With the Shift battle style (the default), when a trainer's Astral goes down you can swap yours in before the next one appears, without taking a hit.
  • Share the Vibes. Every Astral sent out against a foe, and still standing at the end, gets a share. Start a fight with a weaker Astral and switch to your strong one to train both.
  • Watch for the boss moment. At half their team's health, every boss changes the fight. Keep something in reserve.
  • Struggling with a boss? Gain a couple of levels, buy some Watermelon Slices and Water Bottles, or try Easy difficulty for that fight. Options are not part of your save, so nobody will know.

Getting high, wisely​

  • Get high to train. More Vibes, better bags and rarer Astrals make the Come-up and Peaking great for levelling and collecting.
  • Come down for bosses. Wild Astrals hit harder when you are high, your bags fumble, and a whiteout at Ego gone costs twice as much money. A Water Bottle or a rest fixes it.
  • Keep moving. Standing around outdoors with the blotter in your bag leads to licking it out of boredom.
  • Ego gone has secrets. Some Astrals, some places and some moments only exist at the very top. Have a look around, at least once, near a tent.

Chai Tents​

  • Rest at every tent you pass. It heals everything, sobers you up, sets your whiteout point and adds a tuk-tuk stop for later.
  • Heal before a boss's area. The tent next to a boss's stage stays shut until that boss is beaten, and so do the shops there.
  • Spend before risky fights. A whiteout takes a share of the rupees you carry.

Collecting​

  • Wear them down, give them a status, then bag. Low health and a status condition together make most Astrals easy.
  • Peaking helps, Ego gone hurts. Your bags hold better from the Come-up, but at Peaking one in ten fumbles, and at Ego gone three in ten.
  • Check the Journal's Where page. It tells you exactly where, when and how high you need to be to find an Astral you have seen.
  • Visit Banyan. He rewards a growing Journal at 10, 20, 30 and all 41.
  • Look at the map for eyes. An eye marks a place where an Astral only visible when high still hides.

Getting around​

  • Bump into obstacles. If something blocks your path, walking into it tells you which wristband and which type of Astral you need.
  • Keep a mix of types. Field techniques need a party member of the right type. Without a Dissociative Astral you will not float, and without a Psychedelic one the dark stays dark.
  • Follow the hint. The Map tab always shows your next goal and which way to head.
  • Unstuck is free. Trapped yourself behind a subwoofer? Fanny Pack, System, Unstuck.

Your save​

  • The game saves by itself whenever you change area and after battles, and you can save from the System tab any time.
  • There is one save per browser. Export it now and then from Options, especially before clearing your browser data or switching devices.
