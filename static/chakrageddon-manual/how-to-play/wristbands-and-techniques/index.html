Wristbands and Techniques

To get your passport back from the VIP Shaman Council, you need all eight Wristbands of Enlightenment. Each one is handed over by a stage boss after you beat them, and each one (bar the last) unlocks a field technique: a way past something that blocked your path. So the bosses are also the keys to the map.

Field techniques​

Techniques never take up a move slot, and you never have to select them. One fires by itself when you walk into, or face, the obstacle it clears, as long as:

  1. you own the wristband, and
  2. somebody in your party has one of the types it needs.

Without those, walking into the obstacle tells you what you would need. So when something blocks your path, bump into it and read.

tip

Keep a mix of types in your party. A team without a Dissociative Astral cannot float, however many wristbands you have. If a technique will not fire, check your party's types, or swap someone in at a Chai Tent.

A few things to know​

  • Floating: while afloat you cannot run, and in some places wild Astrals live in the water too.
  • Shoving: some subwoofers have to be pushed in the right order, and some ledges jam if you push straight in. If you box yourself in, Unstuck (Fanny Pack, System) puts you back at the area's entrance and rolls everything back.
  • Dark places: before you have the Blacklight UV Pulse, you can still walk them, but you only see a little circle around you.
  • The last band opens the velvet rope behind the Backstage Container, and the road to the temple beyond it.

The Wristbands page​

The Wristbands tab in the Fanny Pack shows your collection.

  • The eight bands are listed on the left, each with a dot of its colour.
  • The one you select turns slowly in fake 3D, seen a little from above, each in its own material: three twisted strands of hemp, rose quartz beads, a split ring with a rubber duck and a bottle opener swinging off it, a greasy cheese-yellow stamp band, black rubber with glowing bones, a lanyard with a laminated pass, a smudge of holy ash, and a hologram whose colours run as it turns.
  • Under it: who gave it to you, a description, and what it lets you do.
  • A band you have not earned is a dark silhouette with a question mark, and tells you whose it will be: "Beat Captain Keta for this one."

With Reduced motion on, the bands stand still.

After the wristbands​

Wristbands also open up other parts of the festival:

  • After the third, Prof. Banyan brings you the Festival Timetable (see The Festival Timetable).
  • After the fourth, the lifeguard at the east end of the beach lets you round the rocks to Fishermen's Cove (see Travel).
  • After the sixth, a tarot reader sets up at the Flea Market (see Juniper's Tarot).
  • Every boss offers a rematch a while after you beat them (see Battles).
