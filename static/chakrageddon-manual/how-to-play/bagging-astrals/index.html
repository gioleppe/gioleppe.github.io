Bagging Astrals

You do not catch Astrals here. You bag them: wear a wild one down in battle, then "stabilise" it in a bag. That is how you make friends at this festival.

How to bag an Astral​

  1. Find a wild Astral and fight it.
  2. Wear it down. The less Serotonin it has left, the better your chances.
  3. Choose BAG and pick a bagging item.
  4. Watch it shake. Three shakes and it is yours: "Bagged!"

If it breaks free, the turn goes on as usual and you can try again. "So close! It wriggles free." means it was a near thing.

You can only bag wild Astrals. Trainers do not take kindly to it.

The three bags​

ItemPriceHow good
Ziploc BaggieRs 60The basic one. Works best on a worn-out Astral.
Rizla PaperRs 180Wraps tighter: 1.6 times the baggie's chance.
Amber VialRs 420Lab-grade containment: 2.4 times the baggie's chance.

What improves your odds​

  • Low Serotonin. An Astral on its last sliver of health is about three times as easy to bag as a fresh one.
  • A status condition. An Astral with any status (Cottonmouth, a K-hole, Ego Death, Jaw Lock) is half as easy again to bag.
  • A better bag. See above.
  • Being high. From the Come-up your bags hold better, and Peaking or beyond better still (up to 1.3 times). But see the catch below.
  • The Astral itself. Common Astrals are easy; rare ones fight the bag; legendary ones are very hard indeed.

To give you a feel for it: a common crab at full health in a plain Ziploc Baggie goes in about one time in four. Worn down to almost nothing, about three times in four. Worn down and with a status, it is practically guaranteed.

The catch: clumsy hands​

The higher you are, the clumsier your hands. When you are Peaking, one bag in ten simply flutters off into the night before it touches anything ("Your hands are not your hands."). At Ego gone it is three in ten. The bag is used up either way.

What counts is how high you were when the battle started. See Intoxication.

Where new Astrals go​

You carry up to six Astrals. When your party is full, a newly bagged Astral goes to the stash, which Chai Mama keeps at every Chai Tent. She patches it up as it arrives, so it is always fit to fight when you fetch it. You can swap Astrals between your party and the stash whenever you sit at a tent (see Chai Tents and Whiteouts).

Give your new friend a nickname from its page in the Fanny Pack, if you like: pick a suggestion, or type one of up to 12 characters.

Special Astrals​

  • High-only Astrals only show themselves to people who are Peaking or beyond. Somebody in each place where one hides will drop a hint, and the Journal and the map mark it with a little eye.
  • Night Astrals only come out after dark.
  • Set Astrals only come out while a particular set is playing (see The Festival Timetable).
  • One-of-a-kind Astrals wait at the end of quests. If you run from one, or knock it out by accident, it usually comes back for another try later. Bring your best bags.

The Trip Journal​

The Journal tab in the Fanny Pack is a duct-taped notebook with a page for every Astral.

  • An Astral you have never met is a row of question marks.
  • One you have seen gets a blue ballpoint sketch and its types.
  • One you have bagged gets its full picture and Banyan-style field notes.
  • The counter at the bottom shows how many you have seen and bagged.
  • An eye next to an unseen Astral means something only visible when high lives somewhere you have already been. The page tells you where: "Get higher to see it."

Press A on any Astral you have seen to open its page. Switch between Where and Notes with left and right (or a tap), and scroll with up and down.

  • Where lists every place it lives, with levels and conditions: by day or at night, during a set, from a certain level of high, on land or on water. It also covers gifts and one-of-a-kind Astrals, and what it evolves from and into.
  • Notes has the full journal entry, once you have bagged one.
