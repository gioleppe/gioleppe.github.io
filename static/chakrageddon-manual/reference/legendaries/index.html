Legendaries and one-of-a-kinds

Spoilers

This page explains how to find every secret Astral in the game.

Six Astrals never turn up in an ordinary patch of incense. Three are true legendaries, and three more only show themselves to people who are very, very high. On top of those, a few Astrals come to you as gifts or one-off encounters.

Legendaries are hard to bag: their catch rates are the lowest in the game (Mandalord, Sunraga and Bassilisk 15, Vicchiomorph 20, Mirrorling and Lucidra 35; a Crabcake is 190). Bring a stack of Amber Vials, wear them down to a sliver of health, and give them a status condition if you can, which makes every bag 50% more likely to hold. Being Peaking or Ego gone also helps the odds (by 30%), but makes your hands shake: a bag can flutter off into the night before it even lands (10% of throws while Peaking, 30% with your ego gone).

The good news: none of the three legendaries can be lost. If one gets away or you knock it out, it comes back.

Mandalord​

Psychedelic / Organic · Clear Head · Lv 37

A sand mandala on the Sadhu Cave's east wall that nobody ever finished. Beat Baba Smoke and he asks you, in smoke, to finish it: four coloured sands, each from a different corner of the map.

  1. Shell Sand (white): Driftwood Beach, on the tide line, at sunrise (5 to 7 AM).
  2. Kumkum (red): Pujari Mohan at the Roadside Shrine, once Lakshmi is off the road.
  3. Turmeric (yellow): beat Line Cook Anil behind the Omelette Stall.
  4. UV Powder (glow): a jar at the Forest Stage that only shows while you are Peaking.

Lay them on the wall sober (Microdose), and the pattern turns and Mandalord steps out. If it slips away, leave the cave and come back. Once it is in your Journal, Baba sweeps the mandala away and gives you a Sand Mala.

The step-by-step is in The Unfinished Mandala.

Its ability, Clear Head, means it can never fall into a Thought Loop or a K-hole. Lead Lakshmi of the Four has one of her own, at Lv 41.

Sunraga​

Psychedelic / Stimulant · Overdrive · Lv 42

The sunset itself. Nobody can see it come out while Team Undercover's fence is up on the Sunset Cliff.

  1. Expose Team Undercover: bring the three memos to the hippie in the Flea Market (Team Undercover's Plan). The quest The Freed Sunset starts.
  2. Be on the Sunset Cliff between 5 and 8 PM while Peaking. If you arrive early, the Sunset Watcher in the south-west will sit with you until 6 PM. The sun stops halfway into the sea, turns round and looks at you from the cliff's edge, in front of the crowd. Reach out to it.
  3. If it drops into the sea instead of into your bag, it will be back at the next sunset ("for the ones who are high enough to see it").

Sunraga is weak only to Bummer moves. Its signature move is Golden Hour.

Bassilisk​

Downer / Bummer · Grounded · Lv 50

The bass under the Mainstage, and the strongest wild Astral in the game.

  1. Finish the game. The Last Drop starts after the credits.
  2. Win all eight boss rematches (rematch list). The Promoter's own rematch only opens after the credits.
  3. Go to the Backstage Container. The speaker stack in the north-east corner is humming a note too low to hear. Reach into the bass.
  4. If it sinks back into the speakers, walk out and back in.

Bassilisk takes almost nothing from Psychedelic moves (a quarter) and resists Bummer. Hit it with Dissociative or Downer moves. Its Grounded ability means it never takes a critical hit, so luck will not save you. Its signature move is Sub Drop.

Vicchiomorph​

Dissociative / Psychedelic · Dreamer · Lv 28

A lanky grey-green visitor on a pocket-sized saucer, with stars in its very big, very black eyes.

  1. Do The Aliens of Vicchio for Mantequilla in the Dense Bamboo Thicket: three Glowing Caps found while Peaking, then a landing at night.
  2. A path opens east through the bamboo to the Vicchio Crop Circle.
  3. The saucer only hovers over the circle at night (7 PM to 6 AM), and only for people who are Peaking. Wave back.
  4. If it zips up into the stars, leave the crop circle and come back.

Its ability, Dreamer, makes it drift out of the way of one attack in seven. Its signature move is Close Encounter. Psychedelic, Organic and Downer moves hit it hard.

Mirrorling​

Dissociative · Mirror · Lv 27–30

A wild Astral that only exists for people whose ego has gone. Float out on the water of the Arabian Sea Shallows while Ego gone (lick the blotter well past Peaking) and it can turn up among the crabs and Neighbula.

Its ability, Mirror, copies the first move used on it into its own moveset for the rest of the battle. Open with something you would not mind being hit by. Psychedelic and Organic moves hit it hard.

Lucidra​

Psychedelic / Downer · Dreamer · Lv 27–30

Like Mirrorling, but stranger. Float out on Arambol Sweetwater Lake while Ego gone, and at night: it only surfaces after 7 PM and before 6 AM. Stimulant, Organic and Downer moves hit it hard.

tip

Ego gone hits hard both ways: wild Astrals come out two levels higher, and a whiteout costs twice as many rupees. Spend your money before you go fishing for Mirrorling and Lucidra, and keep Water Bottles handy to come down a band.

Gifts and one-off Astrals​

These are not legendary, but you only get them one way, or only once.

The starters​

Ganjapod, Jawling and Blottertot, Lv 5, from Professor Banyan at the start: you pick one. The other two are wild, but only while you are Peaking: Ganjapod on Driftwood Beach (Lv 3–4), Jawling on the Sandy Trail (Lv 4–6) and Blottertot in the Poi Pit (Lv 6–8).

Nitroxide, from the Glowing Stranger​

The reward for Peak Experience: a Nitroxide, Lv 25. Nitroxide is also wild in the Scooter Ditch, the Sea Shallows' water, the Dry Ice Corridor, the Sunset Cliff and the Load-In Road, and at the Forest Stage during Darkpsy hour. Its ability, Burst, makes its first move after coming out hit twice as hard; then it pops at the end of its second turn.

The Shadowcop in the Dry Ice Corridor​

Once only, in the fog in the middle of the Dry Ice Corridor. Pay it Rs 500 and a sheepish Shadowcop, Lv 25, drifts out. Refuse and you face a Bribe-asaurus, Lv 29. Either is a wild battle, so you can bag it.

The Big One​

In the Sea Cave in Fishermen's Cove, asleep in Rosa's grandfather's boat (Low Tide): a Lobsterlad, Lv 27, the size of a fridge. Once only.

Your reflection​

Not an Astral to catch, but worth knowing about: at Ego gone, now and then, your reflection waits on Driftwood Beach or the Sandy Trail and offers to fight you, with your own team at the same levels. It gives no Vibes and no prize, and it cannot white you out. Afterwards everything is put back exactly as it was.
