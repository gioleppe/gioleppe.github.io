Places

Every area on the region map (the Map tab in your Fanny Pack), roughly from the start of the game to the end. The map shows the four hubs as red squares (Driftwood Shack Beach, the Anjuna Flea Market, the 5 AM Omelette Stall and the Mainstage Temple) and everything else in yellow, with "???" for places you have not been yet. Indoor places (shacks, villas, booths) appear on the map as part of the area they are in.

A boss gate means the area's Chai Tent and shop stay shut until its boss is beaten. Dark areas are lit only around you until you have the Blacklight UV Pulse.

Driftwood Shack Beach​

The start, and home. A strip of sand on the south-west coast with Banyan's shack, a stall, incense clouds and the sea. You wake up here, and you come back here a lot: to show Banyan your Journal, for the sunrise set, and whenever you white out after resting in his hammock.

  • Chai Tent, and the Beach Stall.
  • Banyan's Shack: a hammock that heals, and Banyan's diary.
  • Set: the Sunrise set, 6 to 8 AM: Banyan stands at the water, waiting for someone. Neighbula comes out.
  • People: Prof. Banyan, the Crab Raver (The Crab Rave), and the Glowing Stranger, but only while you are Peaking (Peak Experience).
  • Exits: north to the Sandy Trail; east round the rocks to Fishermen's Cove (four wristbands).
  • At Ego gone: a young Banyan sits on a log: "Day 3. Two weeks is plenty." Your reflection may be waiting. Banyan's diary has a page you have never seen.

Sandy Trail​

The first route, winding north from the beach. Tide pools and a drum circle round a bonfire south of the creek, one plank bridge, then dunes and incense up to the Poi Pit. Ranger Asha stands at the top and will not let you go north with only one Astral.

  • Trainers: gentle ones (Lv 3–4), the rival's first fight, and Night Owl Suki at night once you have the timetable.
  • Exits: south to the beach, north to the Poi Pit.
  • At Ego gone: a lifeguard whose chair vanishes a second after he does, and sometimes your reflection.

The Poi Pit​

Boss 1, DJ Diabolo. A real dancefloor: a stage with an LED wall, lasers and strobes, poi spinners at its edges, and a packed crowd you have to wade through.

  • Chai Tent and a stall (Beach Stall stock). Boss gate.
  • Set: Diabolo headline set, 10 PM to 1 AM. The whole floor fills up, an MC hypes the crowd, Ravelord comes out, and it is the only time Diabolo takes a rematch.
  • Quest: A Thirsty Spinner.
  • Exits: south to the Sandy Trail; north through fairy lights to the Tantric Yurt (Machete Hack).
  • At Ego gone: the whole crowd turns to stare at you while the music cuts for one bar.

The Tantric Yurt​

Boss 2, Guru Svetlana. Shoes off, ego off. A big yurt of cushions and candles at the north end of a garden.

  • Chai Tent. Boss gate.
  • Set: Ecstatic dance, 8 to 10 AM: gongs, bare feet and a lot of breathing. Chillem drifts in.
  • Exits: south to the Poi Pit, east to the Anjuna Flea Market.

Anjuna Flea Market​

The first hub, in the middle of the map. Stalls, a lassi wallah, a tuk-tuk driver who only takes press, and the best shop of the early game.

  • The market shop, and the Sunrise Guesthouse (a bed for Rs 100 that heals, the noticeboard, and Undercover Memo 1).
  • Juniper's tarot table once you have six wristbands (Rs 500 to remember a move).
  • Set: Night market, 5 to 8 PM: a night vendor with rare stock. Bribe-asaurus comes out.
  • Quests: The Lost Sandals (guesthouse), Team Undercover's Plan, A Collector's Eye.
  • People: the Astral Auntie, while you are Peaking. Insomniac Theo, at night once you have the timetable.
  • Exits: west to the Yurt; north to the Clogged Dirt Road (after Svetlana); east past a subwoofer to the Back Alley (Bass Drop Shove); south through padlocked VIP fences to the Dry Ice Corridor (Jaw Grind).
  • At Ego gone: a stall that was not there a minute ago, selling memories, gently used. The vendor looks a lot like you.

Flea Market Back Alley​

Dark. Three yards of shipping containers between the market and the Omelette Stall, joined by one-tile gaps. The first time you come in, Team Undercover lock you in until you have beaten all seven of them. After that, it belongs to the people getting high.

  • Exits: west to the Flea Market, east to the Omelette Stall.

Clogged Dirt Road​

Dark. A scooter jam round a sacred cow called Lakshmi, who has been lying across the middle of the road since Tuesday. Two detours go round her: the Jungle Shortcut to the west and the Roadside Shrine to the east, each joining the road at its south end and its north end.

  • Quest: A Coconut for Lakshmi gets her off the road for good. "SHE GOT UP. FOR NOW."
  • Exits: south to the Flea Market; north to the Scooter Ditch; the two detours on each side.
  • At Ego gone: the cow gets up and walks off into the jungle, and you can walk where she lay.

Jungle Shortcut​

Dark. "It is not shorter." Five bends of jungle west of the road, a stream with one plank, a dealer's pocket and a coconut grove.

  • Quests: The Lost Tour Group; Pradeep's palms for A Coconut for Lakshmi.
  • Exits: both ends onto the Clogged Dirt Road.

Roadside Shrine​

East of the road. A gate with a guard who wants money, a pilgrims' lane, a long walk past Baba Neem, a marigold garden, and a courtyard with the shrine, Pujari Mohan and an old woman who has lived here for seventy years.

  • Quests: A Coconut for Lakshmi, Flowers Before Dawn.
  • Exits: both ends onto the Clogged Dirt Road.

The Sunken Scooter Ditch​

Boss 3, Captain Keta. A ditch of half-sunk scooters with their indicators still blinking, and a stage with a captain's chair.

  • Chai Tent. Boss gate.
  • Exits: south to the Clogged Dirt Road; north across the water to the Arabian Sea Shallows (Pink Flamingo Float).

The 5 AM Omelette Stall​

Boss 4, Chef Raju, and the second hub. "Open at 5 AM. It is always 5 AM." A griddle, a menu board and a queue that never ends.

  • Chai Tent. Boss gate.
  • Set: The 5 AM rush, 5 to 7 AM: every wrecked raver on the island queues for an omelette. Omeletron comes out.
  • The rival's second fight, just inside the west entrance.
  • Exits: west to the Back Alley; north through fairy lights to the Bamboo Thicket (Machete Hack); south down banyan roots to the Backstage Container (Banyan Root Climb).
  • At Ego gone: a second Raju, half a beat late.

Fishermen's Cove​

A big, quiet bay between Driftwood Beach and the Sunset Cliff: rock pools in the west, a fishing village with boats on the sand, a toddy shack and a fish stall, a palm grove in the east, and two islets out on the flats.

  • Toddy Shack shop.
  • The tide: the flats are walkable from 4 to 10, morning and evening, and sea the rest of the time. Flat rocks let you sit and wait for it.
  • The Sea Cave, in the bigger islet, reachable only at low tide.
  • Quest: Low Tide. Afterwards Rosa rows you to the Arabian Sea Shallows and back.
  • Exits: west to the beach (four wristbands to come this way); north-east up banyan roots to the Sunset Cliff (Banyan Root Climb; coming down is free).

Arabian Sea Shallows​

A wide bay, mostly water: a long sunbathing beach on the west, sandbars and a spit in the east, a rocky islet in the middle with the Sea Grotto inside, and the Sadhu Cave's mouth in the cliffs to the north.

  • Quest: Mad Dogs and Englishmen.
  • Mirrorling swims here, for those whose ego has gone.
  • Exits: south to the Scooter Ditch; east to Sweetwater Lake; north to the Sadhu Cave (a sadhu bars the way until Chloe is beaten); into the Sea Grotto on the islet. You need the Pink Flamingo Float to get around.

The Sea Grotto​

Dark. Inside the Shallows' islet: tide pools, a smuggler, a cave diver, a sign scratched by three different people, and the cool blue clay at the back.

  • Exits: out onto the islet's south landing.

Arambol Sweetwater Lake​

A lake with a small island and a jetty, woods all round. Things swim here that do not swim anywhere else, including Lucidra, at night, for those whose ego has gone.

  • Exits: west to the Shallows (float), south to the Forest Stage.

190 BPM Forest Stage​

Boss 5, Baron Von Darkpsy. Dark. A clearing with a dancefloor, UV light, fog and glowing mushrooms, and trees that pulse to the beat.

  • Chai Tent. Boss gate.
  • The DJ Booth: Selector Nandini and the roadie from Lost Crates.
  • Set: Darkpsy hour, 2 to 5 AM: fog, UV and 200 BPM. Nitroxide comes out to dance.
  • Exits: north to Sweetwater Lake; south to the Bamboo Thicket; the DJ Booth's door in the north-east.
  • At Ego gone: the trees dance.

Dense Bamboo Thicket​

Bamboo, fairy lights and a channel of water between the Omelette Stall and the Forest Stage. Mantequilla crouches in a gap at its east edge, picking mushrooms.

  • Quest: The Aliens of Vicchio.
  • Exits: south to the Omelette Stall, north to the Forest Stage (Machete Hack for the lights, Pink Flamingo Float for the channel), and east to the Vicchio Crop Circle once the aliens have landed.

Vicchio Crop Circle​

Hidden until the end of The Aliens of Vicchio. Wheat, flattened in perfect circles. Nobody grows wheat in Goa. By day it is a very tidy field; at night something hums in the middle.

  • Vicchiomorph, at night, for people who are Peaking.
  • Exits: west to the Bamboo Thicket.

Dry Ice Corridor​

Indoors. A maze of shipping containers and dry-ice foggers between the Flea Market and the Sunset Cliff, with a Shadowcop in the fog that wants a bribe.

  • Exits: north to the Flea Market, south to the Sunset Cliff.

The Sunset Cliff​

Boss 6, Influencer Chloe. A clifftop over the sea with a stage, a ring light, a crowd facing the sunset with their phones up, and the Sunset Content Villa.

  • Chai Tent. Boss gate.
  • The Sunset Content Villa: Assistant Pippa and Undercover Memo 2.
  • Set: Sponsored sunset, 6 to 8 PM: Chloe livestreams the sunset. Sirenowl comes out.
  • Sergeant Baksheesh after the memos, and Sunraga at sunset once Team Undercover is exposed, for those who are Peaking.
  • Exits: north to the Dry Ice Corridor; west down a path to Fishermen's Cove; east past banyan roots to the Load-In Road (Banyan Root Climb).
  • At Ego gone: a second sun that the influencers turn round to film, and stairs of light out over the sea. Come down out there and you wash up on the beach.

The Sadhu Cave​

Boss 7, Baba Smoke. Dark, indoors. Tunnels, candles, smoke, and a faded mandala on the east wall.

  • No Chai Tent.
  • Quest: The Unfinished Mandala, for Mandalord.
  • The Inner Room, through the east wall, only at Ego gone.
  • Exits: south to the Arabian Sea Shallows.

The Load-In Road​

The festival's service road between the Sunset Cliff and the Backstage: a truck park, a security checkpoint, a lighting store maze, a generator yard and a crew catering corner.

  • Crew Catering shop.
  • Set: Generator Disco, 9 PM to 3 AM, once the power is back. Glowkid comes out to dance.
  • Quest: Power Cut.
  • Exits: west to the Sunset Cliff (the roots), east to the Backstage Container.

Backstage Container​

Boss 8, The Promoter. "If you can read this you should not be here." A hall of flight cases, a bar, a table of cash and a big chair.

  • No Chai Tent. The Backstage Bar opens after the Promoter is beaten.
  • The Green Room: two Bottlers and Undercover Memo 3.
  • Bassilisk, under the speaker stacks, after all eight rematches.
  • Exits: north up the roots to the Omelette Stall; west to the Load-In Road; the Green Room door in the north wall; south past the velvet rope to the Last Dancefloor (All-Access Hologram).

The Last Dancefloor​

The victory road. A long field between the Backstage and the Mainstage: a chill-out ring with a welfare tent at the top, three bands of dancing lanes, packed crowds, the sound desk, the light towers, and the barrier at the front.

  • Chai Tent (the welfare tent) and the Merch Stall.
  • Quest: Lost in the Crowd.
  • Exits: north to the Backstage; the gap in the barrier, once the Front Row are beaten, sends you crowd-surfing onto the Mainstage Temple. The temple's back stairs come out at the side gate here.

Mainstage Temple​

The last hub, in the far south-east. A terraced ziggurat behind the stage: a plaza at the foot, five terraces guarded by the Four-on-the-Floor and the rival, and the VIP Shaman Council on the summit.

  • Chai Tent (it closes once you start on the Four, until you have beaten all of them) and the Water Auntie.
  • No wild Astrals, and you cannot call a tuk-tuk from here.
  • Exits: the back stairs down to the Last Dancefloor.
