Side quests

Spoilers

This page gives away every quest's solution and reward. The Quests tab in your Fanny Pack always shows the next step of each active quest, so try that first.

People with a quest to offer have a ! over their head; people waiting for you to hand something in have a ?. A few quests start by themselves. Steps unlock one at a time, and you can often pick up a quest item before anyone has asked for it: the quest then starts as soon as you have it.

The quests are listed roughly in the order you can do them.

Fill the Trip Journal​

Giver: Professor Banyan, Driftwood Beach. Starts by itself when you get your starter.

Bag Astrals and show Banyan your Journal. Each milestone pays once:

  1. 10 different Astrals: 5 Rizla Papers.
  2. 20: 3 Amber Vials.
  3. 30: 3 Masala Chai.
  4. All 41: Rs 5000 and 2 Mystery Powder.

See the Astral Journal for where every Astral lives.

The Lost Sandals​

Giver: a tourist in the corner of the Sunrise Guesthouse (the door in the Anjuna Flea Market). The guesthouse noticeboard mentions them too.

  1. Find the sandals on the Sandy Trail: on the east side, north of the creek. You can pick them up long before you meet the tourist; the quest starts when you do.
  2. Take them back to the tourist at the guesthouse.

Reward: Rs 800, a Weighted Blanket and the Festival Poncho (an outfit). If you later lose the poncho in a whiteout, the tourist gives you another: "I always buy two of everything."

A Thirsty Spinner​

Giver: the Fire Spinner in the Poi Pit's chill-out area, on the east side. Six hours of spinning fire and nobody has brought her a drink.

  1. Bring her two Coconut Waters. Banyan gives you three at the start, and the Beach Stall and the Poi Pit stall sell them for Rs 40.

Reward: a Lucky Rupee (held: more critical hits), "found in the ashes".

The Crab Rave​

Giver: the Crab Raver on the west side of Driftwood Beach. Under the bass there is a second bass: the crabs, who have been raving on this beach since before the Portuguese.

  1. Bag all four crabs: Crabcake, Lobsterlad, Ketacrab and Wonkrab.
    • Crabcake and Ketacrab are wild on Driftwood Beach from the very start.
    • Lobsterlad: evolve a Crabcake (Lv 22, or a Warm Kingfisher), or catch one in Fishermen's Cove, the Sea Shallows' water, the Sea Grotto or on the Sunset Cliff.
    • Wonkrab: evolve a Ketacrab (Lv 24), or catch one in Fishermen's Cove, the Sea Cave, or the water of the Sea Shallows and Sweetwater Lake.
  2. Tell the Crab Raver the guest list is complete.

Reward: a crab dance on the beach ("Claws up! Sideways! No, the other sideways!") and the Crab Suit, hand-sewn from a lilo and two oven gloves. Lose it and the Raver sews you a spare.

Peak Experience​

Giver: the Glowing Stranger, on the east side of Driftwood Beach. They only exist while you are Peaking (lick the blotter until the corner of the screen says so).

  1. The first time you meet them, they give you 2 Amber Vials. Talk to them again and they ask you to bag Banyan's three starters: Ganjapod, Jawling and Blottertot. Your own starter counts.
    • Ganjapod: Driftwood Beach, Lv 3–4, Peaking only.
    • Jawling: Sandy Trail, Lv 4–6, Peaking only.
    • Blottertot: the Poi Pit, Lv 6–8, Peaking only.
    • Their evolved forms do not count: it has to be the first form in your Journal.
  2. Go back to the Stranger on the beach, Peaking again so you can see them.

Reward: a Nitroxide, Lv 25. "This one only lives for thirty seconds at a time. It wants to spend them with you."

Team Undercover's Plan​

Giver: the hippie in the middle of the Anjuna Flea Market. Team Undercover (corrupt guards, dealers selling oregano, influencers fencing off the sunset) want to bottle the festival's dopamine and shut the free parties down. They write everything down.

  1. Memo 1, the Sunrise Guesthouse: beat Undercover Grunt Ravi inside (Shadowcop Lv 14, Mozzquito Lv 14) and he drops it.
  2. Memo 2, the Sunset Content Villa: the house on the Sunset Cliff (after Baron Von Darkpsy). The memo is on the floor on the west side of the room; Assistant Pippa is in there too.
  3. Memo 3, the Green Room: off the Backstage Container's main hall (after Baba Smoke). The memo is in the north-west corner, walled off behind two subwoofers: shove them with the Bass Drop Shove, in the right order. Two Bottlers guard the room.
  4. Bring all three to the hippie.
  5. Sergeant Baksheesh, Team Undercover's head of security, storms up to the Sunset Cliff. Find him by the cliff's east exit and beat him: Bribe-asaurus Lv 33, Sirenowl Lv 34, Dengoth Lv 34, Shadowcop Lv 34, Bribe-asaurus Lv 35 (all Bummer: use Dissociative and Downer moves).

You can find the memos in any order and before you meet the hippie.

Reward: Rs 2000 and a Mystery Powder from the hippie, Rs 1800 from the Sergeant, the fence on the cliff comes down, and The Freed Sunset begins.

A Collector's Eye​

Giver: the collector on the east side of the Anjuna Flea Market. He collects moods, and has never seen a proper Downer up close.

  1. Come back with a Downer Astral in your party. Snoozel, Lassiblob, Duvetghast, Chillem or Neighbula all count.

Reward: a Friendship Band (held: heals a sixteenth of its Serotonin every turn).

The Lost Tour Group​

Giver: Tour Leader Fergus of Gap Year Adventures, at the bottom (south-east) end of the Jungle Shortcut. He had seven backpackers this morning. He has four.

  1. Saskia (the ukulele), low down on the west side: Snoozel Lv 14, Lassiblob Lv 15.
  2. Jonas (the hammock), further up on the west side: Tridog Lv 14, Wooklet Lv 15.
  3. Tim (the opinions), near the top on the east side: Crabcake Lv 14, Mozzquito Lv 15, Glowkid Lv 15.
  4. Beat each one and they walk back down to Fergus (you stand and watch until they are out of sight). Then go back and tell him.

Reward: Rs 900 and a Steel Flask (held: moves cost a third less Hydration). There are only two Steel Flasks in the game; the other is in the Flea Market shop.

Flowers Before Dawn​

Giver: the old woman in the courtyard of the Roadside Shrine. Every morning for seventy years there have been fresh marigolds on the shrine before she wakes up, and she has stopped asking.

  1. Be in the shrine's marigold garden (south of the courtyard, down the steps) between 4 and 6 AM. An old woman in a nightdress is picking marigolds in the dark, fast asleep. Choose:
    • Let her finish: she pads off, still asleep, still humming.
    • Wake her up: she is not pleased.
  2. Tell the old woman in the courtyard what you saw.

Reward: if you let her finish, a Friendship Band that was her mother's ("never wake a sleepwalker, they might be busy"). If you woke her, Rs 500.

tip

To be in the garden at 4 AM, rest at any Chai Tent the evening before and walk over, or just wander until the clock is right. The quest step reminds you that Pilgrim Devi says it happens before dawn.

A Coconut for Lakshmi​

Giver: Pujari Mohan, in the courtyard of the Roadside Shrine. Lakshmi, the cow on the Clogged Dirt Road, is not stubborn. She is offended: nobody has made her an offering since they tarred the road.

  1. A green coconut, straight off the palm, from Coconut Wallah Pradeep in his grove on the Jungle Shortcut (east side, past the fourth bend). "For Lakshmi I climb for free. Well. For a fight." Beat him (Wooklet Lv 15, Chillem Lv 16, Ketacrab Lv 16) and he climbs up for one. If you beat him before the pujari asked, just talk to him again.
  2. A marigold garland from Flower Seller Meena at the shrine gate. Same deal: beat her (Shroomba Lv 14, Chillem Lv 15), or talk to her again if you already have.
  3. Bring both to the pujari to be blessed.
  4. Offer them to Lakshmi, on the road, in front of her nose. Don't do it with your ego gone: at that point the cow on the road is a hallucination wandering off into the jungle, and the real one is not there to take it.

She drinks the coconut water, gets up, one leg at a time, with enormous dignity, and walks to the shrine, where she lies down in the corner of the courtyard as if this had been the plan all along.

Reward: the middle of the Clogged Dirt Road is open for good, Lakshmi's blessing heals your party, and the pujari gives you 3 Incense Sticks and Rs 400. It also lets the pujari give you kumkum for The Unfinished Mandala.

The Back Alley​

Starts by itself the first time you walk into the Flea Market Back Alley (after Captain Keta). Constable Shah pulls a shutter down behind you.

  1. Beat all seven of Team Undercover in the alley, in any order. Both exits stay shut until you do. Their teams are listed in chapter 5.

Reward: the way through to the Omelette Stall, and a confiscated Mystery Powder from Inspector Ashok Desai. Afterwards the alley fills with people getting high.

Mad Dogs and Englishmen​

Giver: Sharon from Basildon, on the beach on the west side of the Arabian Sea Shallows (after Chef Raju, since you need to float to get there). Her party put cooking oil on, "factor nothing". Nigel had a base tan in 1997.

  1. Lifeguard Rohan, on the same beach, knows the cure: cool blue clay in the cave in the middle of the bay. Float to the islet in the middle and go into the Sea Grotto through the gap on its south side. The Cool Blue Clay is at the very back, in the north-east corner.
  2. Bring the clay to Sharon.

Reward: Rs 700 and 3 Electrolyte Sachets, and four bright blue, very happy British tourists. "Go on. Say Smurf."

caution

Once they are blue, Nigel, Tracy and Dave stop being trainers. Fight them first if you want to.

Lost Crates​

Giver: the roadie in the DJ Booth, the hut in the north-east of the Forest Stage. Someone dumped their three record crates around the jungle. No crates, no closing set.

  1. Find the three Record Crates:
    • the Forest Stage, on the east side of the clearing;
    • the Dense Bamboo Thicket, on the west side, south of the channel;
    • Arambol Sweetwater Lake, in the north-east corner.
  2. Take them to the roadie. You get a choice: give them all back, or keep one and sell it.

Reward: give them back and the Selector gives you a Neon Glowstick (held: moves hit 10% harder). Keep one and a dealer later pays you Rs 1500, the closing set is short, and the roadie gives you a look.

The Aliens of Vicchio​

Giver: Mantequilla, crouched in a gap at the east edge of the Dense Bamboo Thicket, picking mushrooms. Mantequilla el Mexicano, from Vicchio, in Tuscany, drove six thousand kilometres in a Fiat Panda because his nonno swore that every year the aliens of Vicchio follow the rave. You can reach him as soon as you can hack through the fairy lights north of the Omelette Stall.

  1. He only talks aliens with people on the right frequency. Go back to him while Peaking. He gives you the Vicchio Antenna, which crackles when a Glowing Cap is near, and lets him talk to you through it.
  2. First Glowing Cap, while Peaking: the north-east corner of the Omelette Stall, "where nobody queues".
  3. Second Glowing Cap, while Peaking: the south-east corner of the Forest Stage, past the big mushroom.
  4. Third Glowing Cap, while Peaking: the east shore of Sweetwater Lake.
    • The caps only show while you are Peaking, and each one only appears once you have the one before it.
  5. Bring the three caps to Mantequilla after dark (7 PM to 6 AM). He lays them in a triangle, plays three notes on his guitarron, and a green light comes down past the bamboo to the east. The canes part, and a path opens.
  6. Go east through the bamboo to the Vicchio Crop Circle and bag the Vicchiomorph. It only lands at night, and only for people who are Peaking. If it zips away, leave and come back.

Reward: Mantequilla's Sombrero ("If they ask, you are from Vicchio"), a new place on the map, and a Vicchiomorph (Lv 28) if you catch it. See Legendaries.

Low Tide​

Giver: Rosa, in the middle of the fishing village in Fishermen's Cove (open from Driftwood Beach once you have four wristbands). At the last full moon party, something came out of the sea and dragged her grandfather Santan's boat, the Maria da Graça, into the cave on the islet.

  1. Cross the flats at low tide (4 to 10 in the morning, or 4 to 10 in the evening) and go into the Sea Cave in the islet's cliff. The flat rock by the tide sign lets you sit and wait for the tide to go out.
  2. Inside, the boat is wedged between the rocks, with something enormous snoring in it. Tap the hull: it is a wild Lobsterlad, Lv 27, "the size of a fridge". Beat it, bag it, or run: either way it leaves the boat.
  3. Tell Rosa.

Reward: Rs 1500 and 2 Amber Vials. At the next low tide the Maria da Graça is walked home and pulled up on the sand, and from then on Rosa rows you up the coast to the Arabian Sea Shallows' beach and back whenever you like.

Power Cut​

Giver: Deepak, front of house, at the generator yard gate on the Load-In Road (after Baba Smoke). All three generators died within ten minutes of each other, and generators do not die together.

  1. Generator one: its cable has been pulled out behind a cable drum. Shove the drum aside with the Bass Drop Shove, squeeze in and push the plug home.
  2. Generator two: bone dry. Find the Jerrycan deep in the lighting store maze, in the south-west of the road, and pour it in.
  3. Generator three: a man in a very clean uniform is "doing maintenance" with a hose. Beat Bottler Gunther (Bribe-asaurus Lv 30, Shadowcop Lv 30, Dengoth Lv 31); he walks off very fast, trying to look like a plumber. Then start the generator.
  4. Tell Deepak the power is back.

Reward: Rs 1800 and Earplugs (carried: your Astrals shake off Thought Loops sooner). The festoon lights come on along the road, and from then on the crew throw the Generator Disco in the truck park every night from 9 PM to 3 AM, where a Glowkid comes out to dance.

The Unfinished Mandala​

Giver: Baba Smoke, in the Sadhu Cave, straight after you beat him. The faded pattern on the cave's east wall is a sand mandala a group of sadhus never finished. Baba explains it in smoke, then holds up four fingers.

Every time you talk to him afterwards, the smoke draws you a map of the colours you are still missing.

  1. White, Shell Sand: "a seashell, and the sun coming up out of the sea." On the west side of Driftwood Beach, on the tide line, only at sunrise (5 to 7 AM): a heap of glittering shell. "By breakfast it will be gone."
  2. Red, Kumkum: "a cow lying in a road, a little shrine, and a red dot." Pujari Mohan at the Roadside Shrine has it, but every offering he has is for Lakshmi until she gets off the road. Finish A Coconut for Lakshmi first, then ask him.
  3. Yellow, Turmeric: "a frying pan, a cook with a cigarette, and two fists." Line Cook Anil is on his break in the south-east corner behind the Omelette Stall (he only turns up once Baba has told you the story). Beat him (Shroomba Lv 33, Lassiblob Lv 34, Omeletron Lv 35) and he steals you a spoonful of Raju's mother's turmeric.
  4. Glow, UV Powder: "a jar glowing purple in the dark, then an eye opened very wide." At the Forest Stage, just south of the stage, a jam jar blazes like a small purple moon, but only while you are Peaking. Sober, it is a dirty jar.
  5. Lay the mandala on the east wall of the Sadhu Cave with a clear head: Microdose, nothing higher. High, the lines swim away from your fingers and you are told to come back sober (a night in a tent, or a few bottles of water). Sober, you lay the colours a quarter at a time: white in the east, red in the south, yellow in the west, and the UV powder in the north. Then the whole pattern turns, and something steps out of the middle.
  6. Bag Mandalord (Lv 37). If it gets away or you knock it out, it folds back into the rock: leave the cave and come back, and it is turning again.

Reward: Mandalord, and then Baba sweeps the mandala off the wall in four strokes ("THAT IS THE POINT"), pours it into the sea, and rolls the last pinch into a Sand Mala (held: no Thought Loops, no K-holes).

Lost in the Crowd​

Giver: Ziggy, at the welfare tent at the top of the Last Dancefloor (after the Promoter). She lost her friend Noor three hours ago.

  1. Find Noor: flowery shirt, round sunglasses, dances like a windmill. She is by the light towers, past the second band of lanes, on the east side, exactly where she promised to wait. Talk to Ziggy first: until then Noor is just a stranger waiting for a friend.
  2. Go back to Ziggy at the welfare tent.

Reward: Rs 1000 and 2 Masala Chai.

The Freed Sunset​

Starts by itself when you hand the three memos to the market hippie in Team Undercover's Plan. With the fence gone, the sunset itself comes out to look, if you go at sunset, and go high.

  1. Be on the Sunset Cliff between 5 and 8 PM, Peaking. The Sunset Watcher in the south-west will sit with you until 6 PM if you are early. The sun stops halfway into the sea, then turns round and looks at you, at the cliff's edge in front of the crowd.
  2. Bag Sunraga (Lv 42). If it gets away, it is back the next sunset (or leave the cliff and come back while the sun is still setting).

Reward: Sunraga. See Legendaries.

The Last Drop​

Starts by itself after the credits. The Promoter is the giver in name.

  1. Win all eight boss rematches. See After the credits for when each one opens.
  2. Something hums under the speaker stacks in the Backstage Container. Go and look at the stack in the north-east corner.
  3. Bag Bassilisk (Lv 50). If it sinks back into the speakers, walk out and back in.

Reward: Bassilisk. See Legendaries.
